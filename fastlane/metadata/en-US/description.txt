Learn American Mahjong with five-minute practice drills: read a rack, practice the Charleston, and make keep-or-throw calls. Every quiz explains why the right answer is right. Four beginner rooms are free, with no account.

Practice alone, between games. Most new players learn American Mah Jongg at a weekly game, then have no way to practice until the next one. Mahj Trainer gives you quick reps anywhere, with no opponents to wait for and no internet needed.

FREE FOR EVERYONE

THE TILE ROOM
Meet every tile family: craks, bams, dots, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers. Then take a quiz on the rules new players mix up: which dragon goes with which suit, what jokers can and cannot do, what a soap really means.

THE CARD ROOM
Learn the card's sections (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons, 369, Singles and Pairs, and more). Then read racks: see 13 tiles and name the section they're chasing. Miss one and a note explains why your pick does not fit.

THE CHARLESTON ROOM
Charleston practice for the part that scares every new player. Learn the rules and strategy of the pass, then work through practice deals: pick 3 tiles to pass and compare your choice with the coach's, with the reasoning explained.

THE TABLE ROOM
Keep-or-throw judgment calls and table rules: when to hold a flower, when to swap for an exposed joker, what an opponent's exposure tells you, and which discards are safe.
