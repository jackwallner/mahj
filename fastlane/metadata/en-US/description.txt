Mahj Trainer is a free app for learning American Mah Jongg with the yearly NMJL card, and it needs no account. Its quizzes explain the reasoning behind every answer, from reading a rack to choosing Charleston passes.

THE TILE ROOM
• Learn every tile family: craks, bams, dots, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers.
• Quiz yourself on the rules new players mix up, like which dragon goes with which suit and what jokers can do.

THE CARD ROOM
• Learn the sections of the card, such as 2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, and Winds and Dragons.
• Read a 13-tile rack and name the section it is chasing. A wrong pick comes with a note on why it does not fit.

THE CHARLESTON ROOM
• Learn the rules and strategy of the pass.
• Pick 3 tiles to pass from a practice deal, then compare your choice with the coach's and read the reasoning.

THE TABLE ROOM
• Make keep-or-throw calls: when to hold a flower, when to swap for an exposed joker, what an opponent's exposure tells you, and which discards are safe.

PLAY A HAND
• Play 13 tiles off a real wall for 12 turns. Every discard is graded, with the arithmetic shown.

ALSO INCLUDED
• The Mahj Minute: five questions a day toward a monthly challenge.
• How to Play, a six-page primer for brand-new players, plus a searchable glossary.
• Practice works offline on iPhone and iPad, with no opponents to wait for.

FREE AND MAHJ+
Free from the first launch: the four rooms (Tile, Card, Charleston, Table), one Play a Hand a day, the Mahj Minute, How to Play, and the glossary. Mahj+ adds unlimited Play a Hand, extra practice sets in all four rooms, Endless Practice with a new rack every time, Fix My Mistakes (your misses come back on a spaced schedule), Game Night Prep (a five-minute session built from your misses), a 90-second Timed Challenge, and the Master Tables for advanced Charleston, defense, expert rack reading, and joker rules. Mahj+ is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, each with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

The practice hands are original and teach the card's sections, so what you learn carries over to any year's card. Mahj Trainer is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League. For official hands, get the current NMJL card.
