Mahj Trainer is an app to learn American Mah Jongg with the NMJL card through short drills: read a rack, practice the Charleston, and make keep-or-throw calls. Every quiz explains why the right answer is right.

FOUR PRACTICE ROOMS (FREE)
• Teaches every tile family: craks, bams, dots, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers.
• Quizzes the rules new players mix up, like which dragon goes with which suit and what jokers can do.
• Teaches the sections of the card, such as 2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, and Winds and Dragons.
• Shows a 13-tile rack and asks which card section it is chasing.
• Practices the Charleston: pick 3 tiles to pass and compare your choice with the coach's.
• Drills keep-or-throw calls, exposed joker swaps, and safe discards.
• Explains the reasoning behind every answer, including why a wrong pick does not fit.

ALSO FREE
• Deals one Play a Hand a day: 13 tiles off a real wall and 12 turns, with every discard graded.
• Sends the Mahj Minute, five questions a day toward a monthly challenge.
• Includes a six-page How to Play primer and a searchable glossary.

WHO IT IS FOR
• Helps beginners learn American mahjong before or between weekly games.
• Lets you practice alone, with no opponents to wait for.
• Gives experienced players advanced drills in the Master Tables (Mahj+).
• Works offline on iPhone and iPad, with no account or sign-in.

FREE / PRO
• Keeps the four rooms, a daily Play a Hand, the Mahj Minute, and the primer free for everyone.
• Adds, with Mahj+ (PRO), unlimited Play a Hand, Endless Practice with fresh racks, Fix My Mistakes on a spaced schedule, a 90-second Timed Challenge, extra practice sets, and the Master Tables for advanced Charleston, defense, and rack reading.

Original practice hands teach the card's sections, so skills carry over to any year's card. Mahj Trainer is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League.
