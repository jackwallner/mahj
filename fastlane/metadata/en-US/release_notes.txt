A smarter rhythm for game night.

Mahj Minute is a new daily challenge. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two rack reads, one Charleston call, and two table judgments. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual game night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Mahj Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.
