Mahj Trainer is a practice app for American Mah Jongg (flashcards, quizzes, hand-matching, Charleston drills, and a single-player Play a Hand coaching exercise). There is no live gameplay, no opponents or bots, and no wagering. Play a Hand grades the player's own discards; nothing plays against them.

CONTENT NOTE: every example hand and deal is an ORIGINAL teaching hand illustrating the stable NMJL category families (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, and so on). No hand is copied from the copyrighted NMJL card. The app is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League; this disclaimer appears in-app (Home footer, Settings) and in the App Store description.

CORE FLOW
1. Launch app: first-time users complete a short onboarding (choose skill level: new to mah jongg / know the basics / played before) and see a brief feature tour. Every onboarding screen can be skipped.
2. Home screen shows a Get Started quick session (mixed multiple-choice practice), a row of training modes, and four FREE practice rooms: Tile Room, Card Room, Charleston Room, Table Room.
3. Rooms hold flashcards, quizzes, hand-match exercises, and Charleston pass scenarios.
4. A free searchable Reference (glossary plus a page per card section) is one tap from the Home toolbar book icon and from Settings.
5. Settings has Rate, Send Feedback, theme, reminders, Reference, How to Play, and Restore Purchases.

MAHJ+ (in-app purchase via RevenueCat)
Membership is branded Mahj+ (RevenueCat entitlement id "pro"). All four beginner rooms above are free and nothing that was ever free has been gated. Mahj+ adds one extra practice set inside each beginner room, the whole Master Tables room (advanced Charleston strategy, a defense drill, expert rack reading), the generated practice modes (Endless Practice, Timed Challenge, Fix My Mistakes), the daily Mahj Minute, Game Night Prep, and unlimited Play a Hand. Play a Hand gives every free player one complete hand per day. Progress and stats are free for everyone.

TRIAL TERMS
Both subscriptions include a 1-week free trial. Payment is charged to the user's Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase (end of the trial, unless canceled first). Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Users can manage or cancel anytime in their device's Account Settings. Terms of Use is Apple's standard EULA (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/); Privacy Policy is at https://jackwallner.github.io/mahj/privacy-policy.

OTHER NOTES
No user accounts or login of any kind. No server backend besides RevenueCat's purchase-status sync. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. The only network-adjacent SDK is RevenueCat (purchase/entitlement status only). Local notifications (daily practice reminder) are opt-in and configured in Settings.
