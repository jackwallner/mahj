Mahj Trainer is a practice app for American Mah Jongg (flashcards, quizzes, hand-matching, Charleston drills, and a single-player Play a Hand coaching exercise). No live gameplay, opponents, bots or wagering.

CONTENT NOTE: every example hand and deal is an ORIGINAL teaching hand illustrating the stable NMJL category families. No hand is copied from the copyrighted NMJL card. The app is not affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League; this disclaimer appears in-app (Home footer, Settings) and in the App Store description.

CORE FLOW
1. First launch: a short onboarding (skill level) and a brief tour. Every screen can be skipped.
2. Home: a Get Started quick session, training modes, and four FREE rooms: Tile, Card, Charleston, Table.
3. A free Reference (glossary) is behind the Home toolbar book icon.
4. Settings: Rate, Send Feedback, theme, reminders, How to Play, Restore Purchases.

MAHJ+ (in-app purchase via RevenueCat)
All four beginner rooms are free. Mahj+ adds an extra set in each room, the Master Tables room, Endless Practice, Timed Challenge, Fix My Mistakes, Game Night Prep, and unlimited Play a Hand (one hand a day is free). Products: Monthly and Yearly subscriptions (1-week free trial each) and a one-time Lifetime purchase.

To test Mahj+: use a Sandbox Apple ID and start a trial from the paywall (Settings > Mahj+ row, any locked drill, or the onboarding trial screen). The onboarding trial screen starts the MONTHLY plan; the full paywall offers monthly, yearly and lifetime. After a few finished practices, a short Mahj+ sheet can also rise over a result (same plan, price, terms, Restore, Terms, Privacy); it says "free for 7 days" only when StoreKit reports the account is eligible for the free trial.

TRIAL TERMS
Payment is charged to the Apple ID at the end of the trial unless canceled first. Subscriptions renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends, managed in Account Settings. Terms of Use is Apple's standard EULA (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/); Privacy Policy is at https://jackwallner.github.io/mahj/privacy-policy.

OTHER NOTES
No user accounts or login of any kind, no ads, and no tracking. Optional feedback (Settings > Send Feedback, and one optional question when a membership is ending) is posted to our own Supabase database with no account attached. Local notifications are opt-in.

IN-APP EVENT: MONTHLY CHALLENGE
Free for every player and open now: a five-question round each day (the Mahj Minute), the same for everyone: three on the month's skills, then two table calls; the best 20 days make the month score. During review the link mahj://challenge opens October's challenge (theme: reading your rack); the same screen switches to November's Charleston rounds, the event's content, on Nov 1 in the player's time zone. It also opens from the Home card, shown from the first visit. A server-side switch can hide the challenge; it is on.

CHALLENGE STANDING: after a round, the app sends the month score (points, days played) with a random per-month code to our own Supabase database, which answers how many players are below it. "Top N%" shows for the top half only. No account, name or device id.
