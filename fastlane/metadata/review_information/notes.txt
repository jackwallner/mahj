Mahj Trainer is a practice app for American Mah Jongg (flashcards, quizzes, hand-matching, Charleston drills, and a single-player Play a Hand coaching exercise). There is no live gameplay, no opponents or bots, and no wagering. Play a Hand grades the player's own discards; nothing plays against them.

CONTENT NOTE: every example hand and deal is an ORIGINAL teaching hand illustrating the stable NMJL category families (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, and so on). No hand is copied from the copyrighted NMJL card. The app is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League; this disclaimer appears in-app (Home footer, Settings) and in the App Store description.

CORE FLOW
1. Launch app: first-time users complete a short onboarding (choose skill level: new to mah jongg / know the basics / played before) and see a brief feature tour. Every onboarding screen can be skipped.
2. Home screen shows a Get Started quick session (mixed multiple-choice practice), a row of training modes, and four FREE practice rooms: Tile Room, Card Room, Charleston Room, Table Room.
3. Rooms hold flashcards, quizzes, hand-match exercises, and Charleston pass scenarios. A free Reference (glossary) is behind the Home toolbar book icon.
4. Settings has Rate, Send Feedback, theme, reminders, Reference, How to Play, and Restore Purchases.

MAHJ+ (in-app purchase via RevenueCat)
All four beginner rooms are free. Mahj+ adds an extra set in each room, the Master Tables room, Endless Practice, Timed Challenge, Fix My Mistakes, the daily Mahj Minute, Game Night Prep, and unlimited Play a Hand (one hand a day is free). Products: Monthly and Yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, each with a 1-week free trial, and a one-time Lifetime purchase.

To test Mahj+: sign a Sandbox Apple ID into the test device (App Store Connect > Users and Access > Sandbox), then start a trial from the in-app paywall (Settings > the Mahj+ row, any locked drill, or the trial screen shown during onboarding). The onboarding trial screen starts the MONTHLY plan; the full paywall offers monthly, yearly and lifetime.

TRIAL TERMS
Payment is charged to the Apple ID at the end of the trial unless canceled first. Subscriptions renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends, managed in Account Settings. Terms of Use is Apple's standard EULA (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/); Privacy Policy is at https://jackwallner.github.io/mahj/privacy-policy.

OTHER NOTES
No user accounts or login of any kind, and no ads shown in the app. Purchases use RevenueCat. Optional feedback (Settings > Send Feedback, and one optional question when a membership is ending) is posted to our own Supabase database with no account attached. Facebook and Instagram ad measurement uses Meta's SDK: the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears once, on the Home screen after the first finished practice (never during onboarding or at launch). The advertising identifier is shared only if the player allows tracking. To see the prompt: finish onboarding, finish any drill (for example Tile Check in the first room), and return to Home. Local notifications (daily practice reminder, members' game night reminder, monthly challenge reminder) are opt-in.

IN-APP EVENT: MONTHLY CHALLENGE
The Monthly Challenge is free for every player: twelve questions on one skill, the same for everyone, with a new set on the 1st of each month. Open it from the challenge card on Home, or with the event's deep link mahj://challenge (a player who has not finished onboarding is taken to the challenge right after it).
