Mahj Trainer is a practice app for American Mah Jongg (flashcards, quizzes, hand-matching, Charleston passing drills, and a single-player Play a Hand coaching exercise). It is not a game: there is no live gameplay, no opponents or bots, no chips, and no wagering of any kind. Play a Hand deals from a shuffled wall and grades the player's own discards; nothing plays against them.

CONTENT NOTE (important for review): every example hand and practice deal in the app is an ORIGINAL teaching hand written by the developer to illustrate the National Mah Jongg League's stable category families (for example 2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons, 369, Singles and Pairs). No hand is copied from the copyrighted NMJL card. Mahj Trainer is an independent, unofficial training app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed by the National Mah Jongg League (NMJL). This non-affiliation disclaimer appears in-app (Home screen footer and Settings) and in the App Store description.

CORE FLOW
1. Launch app: first-time users complete a short onboarding (choose skill level: new to mah jongg / know the basics / played before) and see a brief feature tour. Every onboarding screen can be skipped straight to the app.
2. Home screen shows a Get Started quick session (mixed multiple-choice practice), a row of training modes, and four FREE practice rooms: Tile Room, Card Room, Charleston Room, Table Room.
3. Each room contains flashcards, self-test choice cards, quiz questions, hand-match exercises, or Charleston pass scenarios.
4. A free searchable Reference (glossary plus a page per card section) is one tap from the Home toolbar book icon and from Settings.
5. Settings has Rate + Send Feedback, theme, haptics, sound, daily reminder, Reference, How to Play, and Restore Purchases.

MAHJ+ (in-app purchase via RevenueCat)
Membership is branded Mahj+ in the app; the RevenueCat entitlement id is "pro" for historical reasons only. All four beginner rooms above are free and nothing that was ever free has been gated. Mahj+ adds one extra practice set inside each beginner room, the whole Master Tables room (advanced Charleston strategy, a defense drill, expert rack reading), the generated practice modes (Endless Practice, Timed Challenge, Fix My Mistakes), the daily Mahj Minute, Game Night Prep, and unlimited Play a Hand. Play a Hand gives every free player one complete hand per day. Progress and stats are free for everyone.

OTHER NOTES
No user accounts or login of any kind. No server backend besides RevenueCat's purchase-status sync. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking.
