## Executive directive

Keep the product brand `Mahj Trainer`, App Store title `Mahj Trainer: Mahjong Practice`, and subtitle `Learn American Mah Jongg Cards`. Replace only the US hidden keyword field with the exact 98-byte field below. The visible fields already occupy all 60 available characters, clearly identify American Mah Jongg practice, and support Mahj's best current ranks. Changing them would discard a working control before downstream conversion is measured.

The new field removes `flashcard`, `tile`, and `rule`, whose tracked combinations are weak or wrong-intent, and adds `training` and `education`, which strengthen the literal practice product. Keep `nmjl` only with the existing nominative-use and non-affiliation safeguards. If App Review, counsel, or the rights holder objects, use the exact fallback field documented below without changing any other metadata.

Do not optimize Mahj toward generic mahjong volume. The product teaches American Mah Jongg through drills, original hands, rack reading, Charleston choices, keep-or-throw judgment, explanations, spaced review, and timed practice. It does not provide solitaire, full gameplay, multiplayer, an official yearly card, copied official hands, Riichi, Chinese-rules play, scoring automation, or live opponents.

Replace the current iPhone and iPad screenshot inventories with six canonical frames per device. Preserve the warm cream, jade, coral, gold, and plum visual identity. Remove the eyebrow layer, increase headers from 96 to approximately 160 to 170 pixels, capture post-answer proof states, and compose the iPad set rather than uploading raw UI with large blank areas.

Treat Vietnam, the Netherlands, Japan, Canada, and Russia as diagnostic regional priorities. They are the five largest observed non-US search-exposure markets, but together produced only eight recorded product-page views and no first-time downloads in the available files. Their metadata should qualify American Mah Jongg intent, not amplify generic mahjong exposure.

### Current field

```text
majong,joker,mahjongg,tile,nmjl,charleston,flashcard,tutor,beginner,lesson,drill,rule,hand,strategy
```

The current field is exactly 99 bytes. It is generally strong and preserves spelling variants, but three terms have weak tracked outcomes:

- `mahjong tiles` is currently #97 and the result set is heavily polluted by solitaire and tile-matching games.
- `mahjong rules` is currently #94 and has no Apple autocomplete suggestion.

### Recommended field

```text
majong,joker,mahjongg,nmjl,charleston,tutor,beginner,lesson,drill,hand,strategy,training,education
```

This field is exactly 98 ASCII and UTF-8 bytes. It does not repeat `Mahj`, `Trainer`, `Mahjong`, `Practice`, `Learn`, `American`, `Mah Jongg`, or `Cards` from the visible fields.

The change is deliberately narrow:

- Remove `tile`, `flashcard`, and `rule`.
- Add `training` and `education`.
- Preserve the qualified spelling variants, learner vocabulary, lesson format, card-standard context, and strategy mechanisms.

Do not fill the remaining two bytes. Capacity is not a goal by itself.

### Trademark-safe fallback field

If `nmjl` is not approved for continued nominative metadata use, replace the entire field with this exact 98-byte fallback and make no other simultaneous metadata change:

```text
majong,joker,mahjongg,charleston,tutor,beginner,lesson,drill,hand,strategy,training,education,quiz
```

The fallback removes the organization initialism and adds the literal quiz format. It is not the primary recommendation because `nmjl` currently ranks #23 and is directly relevant to American Mah Jongg card compatibility, but rights and review safety override rank.
