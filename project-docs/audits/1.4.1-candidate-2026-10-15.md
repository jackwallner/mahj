# Mahj 1.4.1 candidate audit

Audited October 15, 2026. This is an audit of the local candidate, not a claim
to have tested a shipped 1.4.1 binary. At inspection, both `project.yml` and
the newest uploaded App Store Connect build identified 1.4.0 (45). ASC had
no 1.4.1 build. The base commit was `5abb9a3`; uncommitted app changes were
copied into a detached worktree before verification because other sessions
were actively editing and building the original checkout.

Scope: iOS candidate changes, purchase/onboarding paths, reminder scheduling,
upgrade presentation, and the existing unit and UI capture suites. Android
and the concurrently edited marketing site are outside this audit. App source
was preserved. The audit did not initiate a purchase or submit a feedback form.

## Findings

### F01. P2, enabled daily and game-night reminders silently expire

New candidate behavior. `Shared/Services/AppSettings.swift:178` limits daily
reminders to 42 dated notifications and game-night reminders to six. Their
switches remain enabled after the requests run out. Only returning to the
app replenishes them. This changes a repeating user preference into a
six-week preference without telling the player.

Reproduced against the candidate's actual `AppSettings.swift`, compiled with
a small command-line probe and constants for its app-only dependencies. With
the last visit on October 15 at noon in Los Angeles, the last daily reminder
is November 26 at 09:00; the last Thursday game-night reminder is November 19
at 17:00. Both plans contain zero notifications after day 43.

Fix: keep generic calendar reminders repeating and show time-sensitive weekly
counts inside the app. That preserves both truthful copy and an ongoing
preference with one request per series. If stopping after inactivity is a
deliberate product choice, make that duration explicit in the preference
instead.

### F02. P2, the CTA experiment records assignment as trial-button exposure

New candidate behavior. `Shared/Services/SubscriptionService.swift:241` adds
`cta_wording` to every nonempty funnel record. `trackPaywallImpression` runs
before prices and eligibility finish loading. Neither checks whether a trial
CTA was actually displayed. An ineligible user seeing only "Subscribe" still
enters the trial-wording cohort. A pitch can also be counted even if prices
never finish loading enough to display a purchase CTA.

There is an additional attribution problem on existing installs:
`ConversionDiagnostics.recordConversion` preserves the first conversion for
life. An older conversion can therefore be sent together with an experiment
arm first assigned much later. The current tests prove stable assignment and
copy, but do not prove actual exposure or conversion after exposure.

Fix: record a versioned first exposure only when an eligible trial CTA is
visible, including its arm, surface and timestamp. Analyze trial starts after
that timestamp. Keep assignment separate from exposure and exclude historical
conversions from this experiment. Evidence is source tracing, not a live
RevenueCat cohort readout.

### F03. P2, upgrade notes and the churn question can compete for presentation

Existing issue still present in this candidate.
`MahjTrainer/Views/HomeView.swift:206` consumes the pending churn moment
before its 0.9-second delay and does not check presentation state again.
`presentWhatsNewIfNeeded` independently schedules the upgrade sheet after
0.5 seconds. On an upgrade with a pending churn moment, both initial guards
can pass, then both sheet bindings become active. Opening Settings or a drill
during the delay is also not checked.

The request is already consumed if presentation is refused or the player
leaves. This follows directly from the scheduled state transitions; the exact
SwiftUI sheet selected in the collision requires runtime confirmation.

Fix: serialize Home presentations, recheck visibility, navigation, scene and
sheet state after the delay, and consume the churn moment when it is actually
presented. Retry after the blocking sheet dismisses.

### F04. P2, the selected purchase plan can change after the purchase tap

Existing issue still present in this candidate.
`MahjTrainer/Views/PaywallView.swift:457` awaits `ensureOfferings()` and only
then reads `selectedPlan` to choose the package. The plan cards remain
enabled while `purchasing` is true. If membership recovery or eligibility
checking suspends, the user can tap a different plan between the purchase tap
and package selection. Apple's confirmation can then name a different plan
from the one selected when the CTA was pressed.

Fix: capture the selected plan at the purchase tap and disable plan changes
until the purchase operation finishes. Verify with a delayed offerings or
membership recovery response. This is source-confirmed interleaving, not an
observed transaction; no purchase was made in the audit.

## Improvements

- **Make capture failures fail the check.** `PaywallRenderTests` and
  `ScreenshotTests` deliberately return without assertions when navigation
  fails. `capture-paywall.sh` checks filenames, but a screenshot of the wrong
  page still satisfies that check. Require the expected purchase heading,
  populated prices, CTA and legal controls before accepting an attachment.
  Emit a nonzero validation result after collecting evidence if necessary to
  avoid XCTest's lengthy failure diagnostics.
- **Before enabling Meta, constrain ATT to the visible Home screen.** The
  candidate keeps both Meta configuration values empty, so this is dormant.
  `HomeView.askForTrackingIfNeeded` checks sheets after its delay but does not
  check the navigation path, scene phase or task cancellation. A player
  opening a drill immediately can receive the prompt over the drill once
  Meta is enabled. Use a cancellable task and recheck the active destination.

## Verification

The pinned-dependency unit run passed **231 tests, zero failures or skips**
on the leased iPhone 17 Pro simulator, iOS 26.5. RevenueCat was 5.80.3 and
Facebook was 18.1.1, matching the original checkout's resolved versions.
See the [unit summary](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/unit-summary.json).

Both purchase capture tests completed successfully, and their recovered PNGs
were visually inspected:

- [Onboarding trial](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/onboarding-trial-test-store.png):
  loaded monthly price and matching trial disclosure, untruncated experiment
  CTA, visible free exit, Restore, Terms and Privacy.
- [Paywall](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/paywall-test-store.png): loaded selected
  yearly price, matching disclosure, CTA, Restore and legal links. The other
  plans are below the initial fold and require scrolling.

These are **Test Store display values**, not evidence of current App Store
Connect prices or actual trial eligibility. The capture explicitly forces
eligible copy. No purchase was attempted.

The broader capture test reached Home, an answered Quick Session question,
a graded Play a Hand discard and an answered Read the Rack question before
automation stalled. Those later image attachments were not finalized. Its
execution trace is retained in [ui-progress.txt](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/ui-progress.txt),
but the full drill capture run is not a pass or visual sign-off. Simulator
commands, including independent screenshots, also stopped responding; only
the audit's own leased device was restarted. A first unit attempt had already
been interrupted for the same infrastructure symptoms before the successful
pinned-dependency rerun.

The **Release simulator build passed**, using a generic iOS Simulator
destination and arm64 architecture after UI automation was stopped. See the
[build summary](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/release-summary.json). The candidate
source still matched the working checkout at final verification. Full local
build logs and result bundles are in `build/audit-1.4.1/`.

The standalone reminder probe passed preservation of the selected 09:00
local hour across the November DST change and replacement of stale weekly
counts with generic copy. It also reproduced F01. The
[retained evidence](1.4.1-candidate-evidence/README.md) includes the probe,
its output, source hashes, dependency pins and read-only ASC results.

Real sandbox eligibility, purchase, restore, renewal and trial notification
delivery require physical-device verification. iPad, iOS 27 and the largest
accessibility text sizes were not re-audited. No App Store approval, shipping
readiness, or full accessibility sign-off is implied by this audit.
