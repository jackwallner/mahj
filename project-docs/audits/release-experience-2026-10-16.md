# Mahj 1.4.0 and 1.4.1: changes and perceived experience

October 16, 2026. Comparison: live 1.3.2 (37), source `6e30385`, against 1.4.0 (45), `437dc62`, and 1.4.1 (46), `acfff99`. The current branch has the same app source as build 46. Jack's priority is what feels different and what could feel worse, rather than a list of implementation changes.

**Decision: hold both builds unchanged.** The feature direction is sensible, but the new trial-reminder screen can leave a large-text player unable to reach either action immediately after subscribing. A free brand-new player can also reach a primer whose Continue and Skip controls are offscreen and unreachable at AX5. Both builds retain a cancellation question after renewal is turned back on. The other concerns below have different certainty and severity; they are not all release blockers or proven regressions.

**The changes most likely to feel worse**

| Change | Who notices | Possible negative experience | Assessment and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| More requests for attention | New and existing players | A trial-reminder offer, upgrade notes, a game-night setup card, native ratings and a cancellation question make a quiet trainer feel more interested in retention. These do not all appear to every player or at the same time. | Product tradeoff. An upgrade with a queued lapse actually showed What's New followed immediately by the departure sheet after Done. Keep each request relevant and dismissible, and give practice priority. Notifications require opt-in; the game-night card can be dismissed. |
| Extra Home cards precede the rooms | Everyone, especially phone users and members without a game-night reminder | The player must scroll farther to get to familiar rooms. The monthly challenge competes with Get Started; members can also get a game-night setup card. | Source-confirmed ordering; annoyance is a product judgment, not a measured retention effect. Review the initial viewport on a small phone before adding another card. |
| The monthly challenge banks every tap and permits one scored attempt | Beginners and accidental tappers | A learning exercise can feel like an exam. A wrong tap cannot be changed, leaving and restarting does not reset it, and the final score cannot be improved that month. | Intentional shared-puzzle rule, disclosed before starting. Keep the first-score rule prominent and frame misses as practice. An unscored retry would be a product change, not a regression fix. |
| The challenge mixes advanced material into a free activity | Brand-new players | Free access can be mistaken for beginner difficulty. The twelve-question set includes defense, rack reads and full Charleston passes, and a low score can feel discouraging. | Product risk. The mix is disclosed and low-score copy offers explanations. Make the beginner Get Started route more obvious than the challenge to someone still learning tiles. |
| Ratings can arrive earlier and more often | Returning players, including people who previously sent feedback | The app now calls Apple's prompt after a qualifying completion from the second launch, with a 30-day cooldown. Previously it required three positive moments, generally waited 120 days after dismissal, and feedback stopped asks. | Intentional change. It removes the custom enjoyment gate, but an earlier interruption is a real experience difference. Apple's display limits still determine whether a prompt appears. Sending feedback no longer ends asks. |
| Corrected streak and weekly counts can decrease | Existing users | An old apparent streak becomes zero after a missed day. Several archived Mahj Minutes completed on one day now earn one practice day, so a previously full weekly goal can become incomplete. | Correctness improvements with a negative surprise. Explain the change in update notes. The Minute still says "Miss one, catch up from the archive", which suggests catch-up can repair missed days even though multiple runs now count once per actual completion day. |
| "Before you go" can be factually wrong | People cancelling, renewing again, or returning after a lapse | A renewed player can still be asked why their trial will not renew. Build 45 can also treat an old cached membership as a fresh departure. | Confirmed observation behavior. Clear a pending departure when renewal resumes, and revalidate the membership when presenting. Build 46 fixes only the stale first baseline. |
| Some old daily results appear missing | Existing users whose system calendar was non-Gregorian | Moving date keys to Gregorian fixes shared daily content, but prior Buddhist-calendar results remain stored under old keys and no longer appear in normal date lookups. | Executed migration proof. Affects both builds. Preserve a way to find or migrate the legacy results. This is hidden history, not deletion of the stored records. |
| Renewing membership can appear as fresh learning | Returning members | Old paid questions re-enter the available mastery totals and create an "Up N this week" gain without new answers. This can make the new progress claim feel unreliable. | Executed against the real record store and ledger in build 46, with identical mastery code in build 45. Separate mastery measurement from access changes. |
| A time-limited run now continues while the app is backgrounded | Timed Challenge users | Looking at another app or being interrupted can consume the remaining time and end the run. | Intentional fix to elapsed-time accounting. Tell the player the timer keeps running before Start. This is limited to the optional timed mode. |
| Paid promotion follows a free challenge result | Free players | "Want more like these?" can feel like a sales pitch immediately after a disappointing result. | Soft, optional upsell; the completed score, sharing and available answer review remain accessible. Avoid making the purchase card the only apparent next action. |
| Longer feedback separates the explanation from its question | Careful readers and beginners | After grading, the auto-scroll can place the question and most tiles above the viewport. Some wrong-choice notes still fall back to a generic section rule rather than explaining this rack. | Observed in [Quick Session feedback](release-experience-2026-10-16-evidence/45-01_quick_session_answered.png): the Odds miss note mentions the even-number exclusion although this Quints rack contains no even number. The rule itself is general; it does not explain why the offered alternative lost. This is a coaching-quality concern, not a proven new rules error. Keep the relevant rack/context accessible and make the comparison specific. |

**Complete player-visible change inventory: 1.3.2 to 1.4.0**

| Surface | New player perspective | Existing player perspective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly challenge | A new free twelve-question activity with rotating monthly themes and the same questions for everyone. It has no timer. | A new Home card and `mahj://challenge` event destination. Prior months become available in an archive. |
| Challenge persistence | Each answer is saved; an interrupted run resumes at the next unanswered question. The last answer banks the score and session/streak credit before Finish. | The first run remains final. Saved results include a skill breakdown, sharing and answer review. TestFlight-era results whose question fingerprints no longer match keep their score but lose review, with an explanation. |
| Challenge review and reminders | Missed-only or all-answer review shows the answer, the selected choice and coaching. An optional next-month reminder is offered after completion. | Opt-in monthly reminders schedule the next three month openings and refresh on launch. A long absence exceeding that horizon can exhaust them. |
| Home | The challenge sits under Get Started. Members without a reminder can set or dismiss their game-night card. | The lobby is longer. Familiar rooms and their locking rules remain; no previously free room is moved behind Mahj+. |
| Weekly mastery | A positive gain chip and progress card appear once there is an earlier day to compare. No gain appears on the first day or a flat/backward week. | "Up N this week" and the strongest gaining room are new. This is a change in questions currently known, not a count of sessions or all answers. |
| Progress and recommendations | Play a Hand accuracy has its own row. A zero best Timed Challenge score is hidden. | Card Room accuracy and recommendations can change because hand-play throws are no longer folded into that room. Rooms that have been attempted can read Learning instead of New even before any question is fully known. |
| Streak and Mahj Minute credit | Streak displays reflect missed days. Five-day weekly credit means five actual practice days. | Stored sessions remain. Old inflated displays can drop; completing several archived Minutes in one sitting no longer fills multiple weekly circles. |
| General feedback | Settings opens an in-app text composer, with no configured Mail app or account required. It reports successful delivery, offline queueing or failure. | Send Feedback replaces the email draft. Offline reports retry on launch and foregrounding; multiple queued reports survive another offline retry. |
| Question reports | Graded pager questions and Charleston passes expose a report link, with the shown question, answer, pick and explanation attached. | Reporting no longer requires describing the whole question in email. Ordinary flashcard decks still lack this link, despite the What's New claim that every graded question has one. |
| Ratings | Apple's own prompt follows qualifying completions, without an enjoyment question first. | Settings Rate opens the write-review page directly. Feedback and ratings are separate; historical feedback no longer suppresses later requests. |
| Trial purchase copy | The free week is promised only when eligibility is confirmed. Otherwise the offer uses plain pricing and Subscribe. | Previously trialled accounts should no longer be promised another free week. Reinstalled members get an automatic entitlement recovery attempt before purchase. Real Apple-account cases still require device verification. |
| Trial reminder | A real newly started trial offers one optional notification before the trial ends, from onboarding or a later paywall purchase. | Restore and paid purchases do not qualify for the trial reminder. It uses the entitlement's actual expiration. A denied notification request no longer causes an unexplained Settings alert later. |
| Onboarding | The main structure remains three introduction pages, skill selection, trial decision, then primer/tour as appropriate. Larger text scrolls; inactive pages are hidden from accessibility; a swipe cannot skip the skill choice. | An existing onboarded install does not repeat onboarding. Members use Continue, and the tour distinguishes a trial from paid membership. The free exit has a 44-point target. |
| Paywall and restore | Large-text layouts let price terms, Restore and legal links scroll with the plans. An initial price load does not flash a connection error. | Plan selection is exposed to accessibility. Restore shows progress and blocks duplicate taps. Settings displays a trial end date and hides subscription management for lifetime members. Monthly onboarding and yearly paywall defaults remain. |
| Departure feedback | Players who turn renewal off or lapse may receive one optional question about the departure. | This is new. First observations should establish a baseline, not infer past churn. The remaining pending-prompt defects are described above. |
| Daily/game-night notification copy | Build 45 can embed the latest positive weekly count in an enabled repeating reminder. | That static count can become false after the player stops opening the app. Build 46 replaces it with generic copy while keeping the weekly count in the app. |
| Generated practice | Cleaner racks and Charleston choices avoid several competing reads; defense explanations consider overlapping number families and runs. Wrong-choice notes refer to the actual rack. | Some answers and explanations differ. The skill interfaces and membership boundaries remain. The monthly challenge adds harder Charleston and defense variants and authored closing questions. |
| Teaching corrections | Lessons clarify dead hands, blind passing/stealing, the call deadline, exposure locking, jokerless payout exceptions, East's opening tile count, visible-copy arithmetic and Charleston direction. | Old wording and some graded choices change. Broad claims tied to card layout, payouts or safety have been qualified. These are instructional corrections, not a new yearly card or playable NMJL hands. |
| Play a Hand | Consecutive-run throws are graded against the best group-value window, avoiding the reproduced arbitrary-discard penalty. | Previously rejected equivalent discards can now be accepted. Saved hand structure is retained. |
| Partial and timed runs | Empty runs no longer receive completion credit; Next is guarded against double advancement. | A partially finished Fix My Mistakes run is scored against attempted questions. Timed Challenge now uses elapsed time rather than sleep ticks. |
| Reading and accessibility | Larger display text, wrapping buttons, scrolling completion/flashcard answers, clearer graded choice state and spoken grading. | Dark filled buttons use contrasting text, gold labels are darker, small tile captions stay on one line, and iPad training layouts grow at accessibility sizes. Ordinary flashcards gain Reveal/Knew It/Again accessibility actions. |
| Motion and celebration | Several question/deck animations honor Reduce Motion; confetti ends its animation timeline after the burst. | Reduced-motion users receive less movement in those components. This is not a claim that every transition in the app has been converted. |
| Update notes and reset | Fresh installs do not get a What's New sheet after onboarding. Reset clears challenge records and the weekly mastery ledger as well as existing progress. | 1.4.0 shows new release notes once. Reset still keeps onboarding and settings preferences; the new records are included in its destructive reset. |

**Confirmed defects and evidence limits**

- Both builds: the actual trial-reminder component clips its title and places both actions offscreen at AX5 on iPhone. It has no scroll view. The normal-text control fits. This was captured by hosting the shipping component with inert actions, not by completing a real purchase. Its source is identical between builds.
- Both builds: the real Brand new onboarding route reaches How to Play after the free Get Started exit. At AX5 the first primer card overflows the screen, with Continue at y=1395 and Skip for now at y=1461 on an 874-point-high iPhone viewport. Six upward swipes cannot expose the footer; XCTest's accessibility scroll-to-visible action also fails. [Screen](release-experience-2026-10-16-evidence/45-new-player-new-player-primer-ax5.png) and [tree](release-experience-2026-10-16-evidence/45-new-player-new-player-primer-ax5-tree.txt). HowToPlayView itself was not changed from 1.3.2, while shared title scaling was changed; this is a confirmed remaining experience failure, not proof that 1.4 introduced the entire problem.
- Both builds: the actual subscription observation sequence renewal-on, renewal-off, renewal-on retains `.trialRenewalOff`. Home consumes the pending value without checking whether the current membership still matches it. This is executed service behavior, not a reproduced real-account cancellation.
- Large-text iPad Home still uses two narrow columns. The actual build-46 Release render on iPad Air 11-inch at AX5 breaks the new challenge label into "CHAL-LENGE", splits room names across several lines and truncates their descriptions. [Capture](release-experience-2026-10-16-evidence/46-ipad-home-ax5.png). The relevant layout is shared with build 45. This is a readability failure, not a demonstrated blocked navigation. Use a single main column at accessibility sizes. The simulator's remote accessibility session timed out, so this check establishes the rendered initial viewport only, not a completed iPad journey.
- Both builds: a real stored Mahj Minute result keyed with the old Buddhist calendar loads successfully but is not found through the new Gregorian date lookup. The store does not migrate it.
- Both builds: recording the free mastery totals one day and member totals the next reports new weekly learning for previously known paid questions. The executed build-46 probe produced gain 1 with no additional answers. The record-store and ledger implementation is identical in build 45. This is a misleading metric, not an observed real customer's renewal.
- Build 45: repeating reminder content embeds an as-of-launch weekly count. Source confirms that it remains static until rewritten; notification delivery weeks later was not observed.
- Build 45: the cached-active/current-inactive observation sequence produces a departure question even if the cached record describes old history. Build 46 adds a five-minute freshness guard for the first baseline.
- The delayed Home sheet code consumes the departure before its 0.9-second wait and does not recheck Settings/navigation/other sheets afterward. The tested queued-lapse upgrade displayed the two sheets consecutively, without a lost presentation. Other collisions or lost presentations remain timing risks, not confirmed defects.
- The trial relay retry defect from the October 15 audit concerns disabled/dry-run marketing measurement, not the customer's practice or purchase experience. It does not drive this report's product verdict.

**Verification and release state**

Final run results and captures are indexed in [the evidence folder](release-experience-2026-10-16-evidence/README.md). Tests exercise the actual historical sources, with the recorded RevenueCat 5.80.3 dependency pin; build 46 also uses Facebook 18.1.1. Exploratory build-45 tests against a newly resolved 5.95.0 are excluded from release evidence.

Both Release simulator builds succeeded. The existing suites passed: 221 tests for build 45 and 226 for build 46, with zero failures or skips. Including the temporary probes gives 226 and 232 total passing unit tests respectively. The probes assert observed behavior and produce captures; their passing does not mean the reproduced defects are acceptable.

Build 45 completed a free twelve-question challenge, resumed at Question 2 after termination, and kept its 4/12 score after termination immediately following answer 12, before Finish. The eight-state legacy capture also completed, and its representative frames were inspected. Both price surfaces rendered with Test Store values. The corrected AX5 new-user journey failed at the primer's inaccessible Skip control. The earlier version of that fixture pinned the skill preference to an empty argument-domain value; its resulting selection failure is an automation defect and is excluded from app findings. The initial six-case UI run contains five passes and that fixture failure, so it is not reported as an all-green UI suite.

Build 46's three UI cases passed. Installing it over the build-45 data preserved the same 4/12 challenge result and its answer review. A free ten-question Get Started session completed, with a question report opened and dismissed without submission. Both experiment arms rendered the expected wording on onboarding and the paywall; the capture test's own four check records all read PASS. Trial eligibility is forced for those wording captures, not verified against real Apple Accounts.

Build 46's Release app also launched on iPadOS 27 with a local member override and AX5 text. Its initial Home viewport was captured and inspected. There is no full iPad, landscape or split-view pass in this audit; the remote accessibility-session timeout prevented interactive verification.

ASC was read without changing release state. 1.4.0 (45) is VALID, ACCEPTED and MANUAL; 1.3.2 (37) remains live. The November challenge event and its combined submission are WAITING_FOR_REVIEW. Its scheduled publication is October 18 at 16:00 UTC, contingent on approval; the old live app cannot route `mahj://challenge`. All 50 version locales have promotional text, release notes and an EULA link. These field-presence checks do not validate every translation or all App Privacy answers.

Real App Store purchases, restore, renewal, notification delivery and hardware VoiceOver are outside the simulator evidence. Structural content tests do not prove every strategic claim against the privately held current NMJL card. No customer feedback was submitted, no paid transaction was made, and neither build was released by this audit.
