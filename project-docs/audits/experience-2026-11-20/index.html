Larger improvements

These are product experiments. The recordings establish the interaction cost. They do not predict conversion or retention.

Let a learner replay one decision

After a coached discard, let the player compare their throw with the coach’s suggestion on the same rack. Restore the saved hand afterward and count the turn once. Test whether a player can explain the difference one turn later. This builds on the original teaching hands and the existing grading engine.

Carry a miss into the next practice

The daily review already shows what a player picked and why. Give an authored missed topic a direct route to a short related drill, preserving room access. For generated questions, offer another example of the skill. Compare second-attempt accuracy and return-to-practice taps.

Make a quiet practice rhythm possible

Try a slower feedback rhythm for teaching rounds: checkmark and coach note on ordinary answers, a larger celebration at a streak or completion. Keep haptics, sound and Reduce Motion preferences independent. Compare how quickly players can locate and explain the right choice.

Give a table-side lookup a shorter path

When a coach names a section or an unfamiliar term, open the matching Reference entry in a sheet and return to the same answer. Start with a few terms used in miss notes. Test whether someone can look up “pung” without losing the rack they were studying.

How to read the evidence

Runtime observations are drawn from iPhone 17e, iOS 27.0, 390 × 844 points. Some clips use debug routes to reach a screen, a local member override, or seeded progress. Entry delays in those clips include simulator automation. Frame repetition or playback smoothness on this loaded Mac cannot establish an on-device frame rate. The audit commit for the app source is 47143b6.

Second pass evidence

iPhone 17e and iPad Air 11-inch simulators, iOS 27.0, the same 1.4.5 (88) debug build. Pauses between actions were cut from the new clips, so their timing shows order, not speed. The host Mac was heavily loaded during capture and several recordings dropped frames, so none of this measures frame rate or launch time. A cold launch held the plain cream launch screen for about two seconds in the simulator. That needs a device measurement before it is treated as a finding.
