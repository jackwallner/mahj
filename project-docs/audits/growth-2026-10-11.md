# Mahj Trainer growth review, 2026-10-11

Scope: what is working, where growth is, what to improve, and whether the Apple
Ads spend is positive. Every number below was pulled on 2026-10-11 from App
Store Connect (sales, subscription event, and analytics reports), RevenueCat
charts (project `proj28030dc2`, iOS only), the Apple Ads API, the App Store
search and review feeds, and the web sources listed at the end. Android
(`projba4fbe38`) is excluded; it went live 2026-10-09 and has no data yet.

## 2. What is working and why

- **The funnel is the asset.** 28% of downloads start a trial and half of
  those pay. That is the onboarding trial page (monthly, one tap, zero-shift
  CTA) plus a product that proves itself in the first session. Do not touch
  onboarding, the trial length, the monthly-first CTA, or the free-beginner
  split. Every growth idea below adds around this, never inside it.
- **Monthly at $9.99 retains.** 75% first renewal against a 51-60% benchmark,
  one recorded paid cancellation, zero refunds. The weekly game-night rhythm
  (Game Night Prep, Mahj Minute, Fix My Mistakes) gives a reason to come back
  that a lesson app lacks once the lessons are done.
- **Trust.** The category's worst reviews are "the bot is rigged" and "it let
  me do something illegal" (Maylee, Mahj Parlour, Eight Bam this month). Mahj
  has no bots, grades with arithmetic the player can check, and corrected
  flagged content in 1.3.1. Its only 1-star review was a content error, already
  fixed and answered.
- **Pricing is right for the segment.** Market mode is $9.99/month and $29.99
  to $39.99/year. Higher price correlates with higher conversion in the RC
  data, and Mahj's numbers confirm it. Yearly barely sells now (2 trials in
  Sep) because onboarding sells monthly. With observed monthly retention, a
  monthly subscriber is worth more than $39.99; leave yearly where it is.
- **Word of mouth has started.** "mahjong trainer" searches convert at a 43%
  tap-through rate on ads, and brand terms ("mahj trainer", "majong trainer")
  appear in the impression-share report. People hear the name at the table.

## 4. Apple Ads: positive, but starved

Lifetime Mahj spend is about $47. Every converting dollar came from one
keyword.

| Campaign | Dates | Spend | Taps | Installs | CPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Practice Intent | Aug 17-19 | $4.24 | 3 | 1 | $4.24 |
| Women 45+ Practice | Sep 26 to Oct 5 | $26.73 | 24 | 29 | $0.92 |
| Practice Exact 2610 (running, $5/day, US+CA) | Oct 8 on | $15.46 | 14 | 7 | $2.21 |

The "Women 45+" campaign's demographic ad group spent nothing; Apple only
serves demographic targeting to users who allow personalized ads, so it cut
reach to zero. All delivery came from the untargeted ad group. Do not use
demographic targeting again.

Keyword detail (both recent campaigns, 16 exact-match terms at a $1.50 bid):

- `mahjong trainer`: $38 spend, 36 taps, 35 installs, 43% tap-through. This is
  people searching the name. With the clone and the #10 organic rank, the ad is
  what puts Mahj first for its own name.
- `mahjong practice`, `american mahjong practice`, `learn mahjong`, `mah
  jongg`, `american mahjong`, `nmjl card`: 1 to 31 impressions each, 0 to 1
  taps. The $1.50 bid is losing those auctions (SplitMetrics US Education CPT
  benchmark Apr-Jun 2026 is $1.93; Games $3.02). Impressions, not budget, are
  the constraint: the campaign spends about $4 of its $5 daily cap.
- Impression share report: `mahj trainer` 71%, `majong trainer` 30%,
  `mahjong` (CA) 0%. Nothing else registers.

### Unit economics

Per trial start, resolved: 50% pay $9.99 (Apple small business rate leaves
$8.49). Observed first renewal 75%. Assume every later renewal at 75%
(conservative; later renewals usually improve): expected paid months = 1/(1 -
0.75) = 4, so $34 proceeds per converter and $17 per trial start. Trial starts
are 28% of downloads, so **about $4.75 proceeds per download**, with $1.19 of
it arriving on day 7. Even at a $3 CPI the ads return 1.6x inside the first
year, and the cash loop closes in under two months. Current CPIs are $0.92 to
$2.21.

### What to change

1. **Keep the running campaign. Raise bids on the intent cluster** to $2.50 to
   $3.00 for two weeks (`mahjong practice`, `american mahjong practice`,
   `learn mahjong`, `learn american mahjong`, `mahjong lessons`, `mahjong for
   beginners`, `charleston mahjong`). Stop at a $3.50 blended CPI. Leave the
   brand terms at $1.50; they already win.
2. **Add the brand-defense terms** as exact match: `mahj trainer`, `majong
   trainer`, `mahjongg trainer`, `mahj trainer app`, `mahjong trainer app`.
   These are the searches the clone is intercepting.
3. **Add a Discovery ad group** (broad match on `american mahjong practice`,
   `learn american mahjong`, `mahjong lessons`) with negatives `solitaire`,
   `free`, `riichi`, `chinese`, `3d`, `tiles match`, `connect`. Use the
   search-terms report after two weeks to promote winners into the exact group.
4. **Raise the daily cap to $15.** It is a ceiling, not a target; spend
   follows impressions. Expect $8 to $12 a day at the new bids.
5. **Turn on Apple Ads attribution in RevenueCat** so trials and revenue can be
   read by keyword (`Purchases.shared.attribution.enableAdServicesAttributionTokenCollection()`
   is not called today; RC shows "No Attribution" for every customer). One line
   of code, and it is the only way to measure keyword LTV rather than CPI.
6. Do not add Canada or Australia yet. CA search volume is a quarter of US at
   $12.99/month and the brand searches are US. Revisit after the spring card.

Watch for: the 7-day trial means paid signal lags installs by a week, so judge
a bid change on two weeks, not two days.

## 5. Growth opportunities, ranked

### 5.1 Ratings volume (largest lever, cheapest)

820 customers produced 21 ratings (2.6%). The funnel is right (enjoyment gate,
unhappy players go to feedback), but the yes branch sends people out of the
app to a write-review page. Written reviews are rare; star ratings are what
the search listing shows and what the ranking algorithm weighs.

- On "Yes, I'm enjoying it", call `requestReview()` immediately (one tap,
  in-app, no typing). Keep the "Rate on the App Store" link as the secondary
  button on the same screen for people who want to write. Apple throttles
  `requestReview()` to three a year per device, which is irrelevant for a
  single ask.
- Add a second trigger at a stronger positive moment: the first paid renewal
  (day 37) or the first Mahj Minute perfect score, for players whose last
  outcome was "Not now". Same sheet, same cooldown rules.
- Target: 100 ratings by the spring card. At that count the listing stops
  reading as unproven beside the clone's 136.

### 5.2 Get on the lists people and models read

Mahj Trainer is missing from Bam Good Time's 2026 app list and from Mahjong
Playbook. Write to both (and to the MAHJ-style teacher community) with a
one-paragraph pitch and a promo code. Cost: an email. These pages feed the
google.com and ChatGPT referrals that already deliver 8% of downloads.

### 5.3 Publish the reference as web pages

`ReferenceContent` (glossary plus a page per card section, all original) is
exactly the content people search for on the web: "what is a soap tile",
"charleston rules american mahjong", "how to read the mahjong card". Export it
to `docs/reference/` as static pages with the App Store badge. The site
already converts (19 downloads from google.com in September with nothing but
a landing page). This is the only acquisition channel that compounds and
costs nothing per install.

### 5.4 Word of mouth inside the app

Clubs and weekly games are how this audience recruits. Two small hooks:
a "Send to your table" share at the room-mastered celebration and after a
Mahj Minute result (the result share exists; add the app link to it), and a
"Teaching a class?" row in Settings that shares a deep link into the Tile
Room. The MAHJ reviews are full of "I sent this to everyone at my table" and
"I teach lessons and use this"; give them the button.

### 5.5 Spring card season (plan now, ship in March)

The NMJL card ships late March to early April every year, and everyone,
including experienced players, relearns it. Eight Bam's whole monetization is
the yearly card. Mahj cannot copy the card, but it can own the moment:

- Ship 1.4 in the last week of March: "New card week" path (how to read a new
  card's lines, which sections moved, a re-mastery pass through the Card Room
  with mastery rings reset to Learning for one week), plus a What's New sheet.
- Raise Apple Ads bids and cap from mid-March through April; search volume
  for everything mahjong peaks then.
- Schedule a win-back offer (section 6.4) to land the same week for lapsed
  trials and lapsed monthlies.

### 5.6 Android cross-mention

Android went live 2026-10-09. Add the Play badge to `docs/index.html` and
mention it in the App Store description's last paragraph ("Also on Android").
MAHJ reviews say "my Samsung friends are looking for you"; the tables are
mixed-platform.

## 6. Retention for trials and Mahj+ members

Facts from the subscription event report (Aug 1 to Oct 10):

- 110 trial cancellations. 57 (52%) were cancelled on day 0, 24 on days 4 and
  5, the rest spread. RC's 2026 report puts the industry at 55% on day 0, so
  Mahj is normal here. Day-0 cancellers started the trial to look, not to
  buy; they are the free tier's audience and they still get the four rooms.
- Days 4 and 5 are the reachable group: they used it most of a week and then
  decided against. That is a value-demonstration problem, not a price problem.
- Paid churn so far is one cancellation. Too early to read; the first monthly
  cohort is two renewals old.

### 6.1 Trial welcome path

Today a new trial goes to the feature tour. Route it instead into Game Night
Prep setup: "When is your game?" sets the weekly reminder and asks for
notification permission in context, then opens the first prep session. The
weekly reminder is the retention mechanic that matches how the audience
plays, and only members have it. Research (RC 2026) says the first session
is the retention mechanism; this makes the first member session the one
feature that recurs.

### 6.2 Day-5 proof

On day 5 of a trial, replace the home header once with a mastery delta:
"Since Tuesday: Charleston Room Learning to Solid, 48 reps, 11 misses fixed."
Everything needed is in `PracticeRecordStore`. The days-4-5 cancellers are
deciding whether it did anything; show them.

### 6.3 Weekly recap for members

A local notification on Sunday evening (or the evening after game night) with
the same delta, plus one line of what is due. Local only, no server. Pair it
with the game-night reminder permission so it never asks twice.

### 6.4 Win-back offer for lapsed trials

106 trials have expired without paying and about 50 more will each month.
Apple promotional offers are available to anyone who has had a subscription,
including an expired trial. Create one in ASC ("First month $4.99"), show it
once on the paywall to lapsed-trial players, and time a second wave to the
spring card. Nothing else in the app changes.

### 6.5 Content cadence

Members have 5 Plus drills, the Master Tables, and the generators. The
generators prevent "ran out", but authored content is what What's New can
announce. Commit to one new Master Tables set per quarter and say so in the
paywall ("new sets every season"), which is also the renewal reason a yearly
plan needs.

### 6.6 Measure which member feature retains

There is no usage measurement at all. Set RevenueCat subscriber attributes
(no new SDK, no account): `game_night_set`, `mahj_minute_days`,
`hand_play_count`, `last_room`. RC charts can then segment churn by attribute
and the next retention decision stops being a guess. This stays inside the
existing "no account, no cloud" promise because the attributes are counts,
not content.

## 7. Feedback prompt: yes, but targeted

The existing funnel is already the research-backed shape: contextual (after a
finished drill), one question, unhappy players never see a rating ask. The
sources agree that behavior-triggered single-question prompts get 15 to 35%
response rates against 1 to 3% for untargeted ones, and that a generic "after
N uses, tell us what you think" prompt competes with the rating ask and
trains dismissal. So do not add a general feedback prompt. Fix the leak and
add three precise moments instead:

1. **Fix the leak.** The "Not really" branch opens Mail with a draft. Many
   players have no Mail account, and the sheet already handles that case with
   a copy-the-address fallback, which nobody follows. Submit in-app: a text
   field that POSTs to a small endpoint (a Supabase table on the existing
   project, no auth, rate limited) with the app version and the last drill id.
   Keep Mail as the fallback, not the primary.
2. **Trial expired, no conversion.** Next launch, once: "What held you back?"
   with four chips (Price, Didn't use it enough, Free rooms were enough, Not
   what I expected) and an optional text field. These 106 people are the most
   informative group in the business and have never been asked.
3. **Auto-renew turned off.** `CustomerInfo.willRenew` is readable; when it
   flips to false for a paying member, ask once: "What made you cancel?" with
   the same chip pattern. Cancellation reasons from Apple's report are
   useless ("Canceled"); this is the only way to get them.
4. **Per-question "Something wrong?"** link on every answer screen, opening
   the same text field with the question id prefilled. The only 1-star review
   was a content error; the 1.3.1 audit fixed several. A one-tap report
   catches the next one before it reaches the App Store.

## 8. Do not do

- Do not add bots, opponents, or multiplayer. The category's lowest reviews
  are rigged AI and rule bugs, and Mahj's structural advantage is that it
  cannot have them.
- Do not change the onboarding trial page, the monthly CTA, the free split, or
  prices. The funnel is 3x benchmark.
- Do not push yearly to monthly subscribers yet. Expected monthly value exceeds
  $39.99; revisit with month-6 retention in January.
- Do not add a second general-purpose prompt of any kind on top of the review
  ask.
- Do not use demographic targeting in Apple Ads.
- Do not copy the card or imply the app includes it, at any point of the
  spring plan.

## Addendum, 2026-10-11 evening: the clone, LTV revisited, and what shipped

**Mahjong Trainer: Learn & Win (Cedar Theory), walked on a device.** Its
138 ratings are star-only: the review feed holds three written reviews, all
2 to 4 stars. That ratio only happens when Apple's native `requestReview()`
prompt fires on warm moments and nobody is sent to the App Store page. Its
listing copy is not copied from Mahj Trainer (different structure, different
features: units, XP, levels, Lesson of the Day), so there is no DMCA case.
First-launch flow: 16 onboarding screens, a 5-question placement quiz, a
"remind me 2 days before the trial ends" step, then a paywall with no skip:
7 days free then $7.99 a week, or $59.99 a year. The description's "free
lessons" are not reachable on first launch. A placeholder tile reading
"JOKER ASSET PENDING" ships in the quiz. Copy the native star prompt and the
trial-end reminder offer. Do not copy the pricing or the hard paywall.

**LTV, honestly.** The $4.75 per download above assumes 75% renewal holds
every month. Only two paid months of data exist (no subscriber has reached a
third paid period yet). Bear case, with benchmark 60% renewal after month 2
and ad-driven installs starting trials at half the organic rate: about
$1.80 per download. Brand-term ads at $0.92 CPI are safe either way; generic
intent terms are not safely positive above a $2.00 bid until the 1.3.2
attribution build shows trial rates per keyword. Revised ask: intent bids
$2.00, $10 a day cap, revisit at 30 days.

**Win-back.** Start a promotional offer at $4.99 for the first month, shown
once in-app to lapsed trials; drop to $2.99 if under 5% take it in 60 days.
Add a non-renewing 3-month pass at $14.99 beside it for the "$12 a month is
too much" crowd. Neither created yet; both are ASC changes Jack approves.

**Shipped in 1.4.0 (branch `feat/retention-1.4`, build 38 to TestFlight):**
in-app feedback to a Supabase table with an offline queue; "Yes, I'm
enjoying it" fires the native star prompt; "Something wrong with this
question?" under every graded pager answer; one churn question when a trial
or membership turns toward the exit; weekly "questions locked in" on Home,
Your Progress and in the reminder bodies; a game night card for members
without a reminder; a trial-end reminder offer after an onboarding trial.
Details: `.claude/rules/feedback-and-retention.md`.

**Site.** Six Learn guides (how to play, the nine sections, the Charleston,
jokers, scoring and table rules, glossary) with Article, FAQ and breadcrumb
schema, a sitemap and robots.txt, generated from the app's reference content
by `scripts/generate-learn-pages.py`. Live at
`https://jackwallner.github.io/mahj/learn/`.
