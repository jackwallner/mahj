# Meta ad pack research (2026-10-15)

Research behind the Mahj Trainer Meta (Facebook + Instagram) app-install pack.
Read in Jack's signed-in Chrome and on the open web on 2026-10-15. Quotes are
kept under 15 words and paraphrased elsewhere. Numbers marked "vendor" come
from agencies or tool sellers, not Meta.

## The 3 findings that shaped the pack

1. **A direct competitor is already buying this audience with "Mahjong
   Trainer" in its name.** "Mahjong Trainer: Learn and Win" has run about 140
   Meta ads since Sep 7 2026 (Ad Library, query "Mahjong Trainer"), all with
   the same primary text ("Think Duolingo... but for American Mahjong") and
   35 to 55 second UGC-style videos of women talking to camera, plus short-lived
   statics ("Play Mahjong every week. Still too slow?", "Can a joker go in a
   pair?"). Its statics died within 4 to 7 days; the videos are the survivors.
   Consequences: every ad here says **"Mahj Trainer"** exactly and shows the
   app's own cream-and-jade screens and tile art in the first second, so it
   cannot be mistaken for the other app; we do not reuse "still too slow" or
   the joker-pair quiz; and the pack's video is a real screen recording, not a
   talking head, so it competes on a different axis.
2. **The paying content in this niche sells solo practice for the gap between
   game nights.** The biggest American Mah Jongg advertisers by volume are not
   apps: Mahj Mastery (37 active ads since Jun 11 2026), "American MAH JONGG"
   (20), Jessica Williams (15) and Mahj Academy all sell printable solo-practice
   systems ("Mock Charlestons. Hand Drills. Pivot Training."). Their copy is
   long first-person stories with the same pains new players post in Facebook
   groups: once-a-week play that starts from scratch each time, freezing in the
   Charleston, not knowing which hand to chase, the new card each April. That
   is exactly what Mahj Trainer does in-app, so our motivations are proven
   demand; we beat them on format (a free app with instant feedback, not a PDF)
   and keep copy short because their long stories already own that lane.
3. **The feed is flooded with mahjong solitaire, so "American Mah Jongg" and a
   real rack must be visible in second one.** A plain "mahjong" search returns
   about 21,000 active US ads, almost all tile-matching solitaire games (Vita
   Mahjong, Mahjong Breeze, Vigor Mahjong "Designed for Seniors, big font,
   large tiles"). Our buyer has learned to scroll past "mahjong game" ads.
   Every ad here opens on the words American Mah Jongg (or a 13-tile rack with
   a joker and flowers, which no solitaire game shows) and never on a tile
   pile.

## 2. Meta creative specs and guidance

Official (Meta Ads Guide and Business Help Center, read 2026-10-15):

- **Feed image and video:** 4:5 recommended, 1440x1800 recommended resolution
  (minimum width 600). We ship 1080x1350, which Meta accepts at 4:5. Primary
  text 50 to 150 characters, headline 27 characters recommended.
  (facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image, /video)
- **Carousel:** 2 to 10 cards, 1:1 at 1080x1080 is Meta's listed default;
  primary text 80, headline 20, description 18 characters recommended. All
  cards take the first card's ratio. We render 4:5 cards as asked and a 1:1
  set from the same source, with the text inside the centre square so either
  crop reads. (facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/carousel)
- **Safe zone (9:16 in Stories, Reels, Feed):** keep the top, bottom and side
  edges free of text and logos; on taller phones Meta may zoom and crop outside
  the safe zone; with disclaimers on Reels keep the bottom 40% clear.
  (facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490). Meta does not publish pixel
  numbers on that page; the widely used Ads Manager overlay values are about
  14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides on Reels (moda.app, billo.app, adkit.so,
  vendor). This pack uses a stricter box for all 9:16 text: x 90 to 990,
  y 290 to 1240 on 1080x1920.
- **Video:** captions "optional, but recommended"; H.264, AAC. Older Meta
  guidance: put the brand and the main message in the first 3 seconds and
  design for sound off (archived facebook.com/business/inspiration, 2020).
- **Advantage+ creative enhancements** (text improvements, music on statics,
  image expansion, touch-ups) are reported to default ON for App Promotion in
  2026 (adlibrary.com, alexneiman.com, vendor). Each is a separate toggle
  (developers.facebook.com, Advantage+ creative docs). Turn off any that
  rewrite text or crop: our type is sized and placed for 50+ eyes.
- **Andromeda** groups similar ads under one entity, so near-duplicates split
  one auction slot (segwise.ai, tryatria.com, jetfuel.agency, vendor). A new
  entity needs a new motivation, format and opening, not a new colour or
  headline. Fatigue is reported at 2 to 3 weeks (vendor). Jack's research
  doc quotes a Higgsfield staffer: same script plus new face equals same ad.
- **Audience:** under Advantage+ audience only location and minimum age are
  hard controls; gender and maximum age are suggestions (jonloomer.com).
  "Women 40+" as a hard rule needs the "further limit the reach" / original
  audience switch in the ad set.

## 3. Reaching women 40 to 70 on Facebook and Instagram

- People 50+ appear in about 15% of online ad images though they are 46% of
  US adults; negative portrayals fell from 28% (2018) to 10% (2023)
  (AARP 2019 and 2024 portrayal studies, aarp.org). 70% of women 50+ would
  consider switching to a brand that represents their age (AARP via
  creativebrief.com). Women report feeling invisible in ads from about 52
  (Lloyds Banking Group via thedrum.com). Implication: show competence and a
  social life (game night, the club, the group chat), never frailty, "seniors"
  or "keep your brain young".
- Readability: HHS Health Literacy Online recommends 16px minimum body text and
  19px for older audiences. A 1080px ad renders about 390pt wide on a phone, so
  1px of canvas is about 0.36pt: this pack sets no text below 44px (about
  16pt on screen) and headlines at 84 to 132px, dark ink on cream (contrast
  above 12:1).
- Real faces: the long-running niche ads use lo-fi photos of real-looking older
  women; competitor Maylee uses a doll meme. Jack's rule bans AI-generated
  people and we have no licensed photos, so this pack wins on the other
  proven lane: real product on screen (Vigor, Word Trip and Mahj Parlour
  run product-only and last months).
- Social proof: Mahj Trainer has 24 US ratings at 4.6 (iTunes lookup,
  2026-10-15), too few to lead with a star count. Real written reviews exist and
  are quoted verbatim in one candidate; no invented quotes.
- Club culture: competitor reviews and FB posts frame the app as practice "in
  the in-between times" for a real table with friends. Ads should promise
  table confidence, not replace the table.

## 4. What new players actually say (paraphrased)

Sources: 1,006 recent US App Store reviews of 8 American Mah Jongg apps
(iTunes RSS: Mahjong 4 Friends, Real Mah Jongg, American Mahjong Practice,
The Mahjong App, Maylee, Mahj Parlour, MAHJ: Learn American Mahjong, The Mahj
App), public Facebook posts found through Facebook search, and the four real
reviews of Mahj Trainer. Reddit (r/Mahjong) is blocked to both the web
fetcher and the browser extension, so it is not covered.

Term counts across the 1,006 reviews: practice 241, card 187, friends 80,
beginner 63, new card 30, Charleston 22, confident/confidence 15, slow 10,
confused 9.

| Pain or wish | How they put it (paraphrase, short quotes in quotation marks) |
|---|---|
| Slowing the table | Practicing alone so they don't annoy experienced players with slow play; worried about "holding up the Charleston"; a 101-class member felt others getting annoyed at how slow her group was |
| Charleston stress | "so stressed out about the Charleston"; wants to practice alone so she isn't overwhelmed with others |
| Seeing hands | The worst part of live play is the time it takes to "see" possible hands; the app helps her spot them quicker |
| Overwhelmed by the card | Learned in person but was overwhelmed by the card; the card "looked like a foreign language" (Maylee ad) |
| Lost after the class | Went to a class and was completely lost; needs to keep learning after a 101 class or she will lose it because life is busy |
| Pivoting | Struggled with when to scrap one hand for another |
| New card each April | Uses apps to learn each new card faster than waiting for in-person game days |
| Intimidation and embarrassment | Wants to review basics so she doesn't embarrass herself; intimidated because everyone seemed experienced; wanted to learn at her own pace, not in an intimidating group |
| Family | A daughter taught her; at 68 she was not sure she was teachable |
| Eyesight | Large, bold text keeps an older player in the game |
| Accuracy matters | Mahj Trainer's only 1-star review says it gave incorrect information about calling jokers; every rule in an ad must be checked |

Teachers and organizers post the other side: a student who does not practice
cannot remember the suits or whose turn it is.

Real Mah Jongg trend context: Yelp reports searches for mahjong clubs up
almost 4,500% year on year (NPR, 2026-04-29); clubs are mostly women.

## 5. Rules carried into the pack

- Name the game in second one ("American Mah Jongg") and show a real 13-tile
  rack, never a solitaire pile.
- One motivation per ad, each a different first frame (Andromeda).
- Promise confidence at a real table; never "seniors", "brain health" or
  memory claims.
- Type 44px minimum, headlines 84px and up, cream background, ink text.
- Real app captures and the app's own tile art only. No AI people, no stock.
- No card hands, no prices, no em dashes, "Mahj Trainer" spelled exactly, and
  "not affiliated with the NMJL" wherever the card is mentioned.
