# Mahj Trainer Meta ad pack: the final 5

Ready-to-upload creative for one Meta app-install campaign (US, women 40+,
about $20/day, installs first). Nothing has been uploaded and nothing spent.
Why these five: `scoring.md`. Evidence behind them: `research.md`. Who they
are for: `personas.md`.

Files live in iCloud Drive, one folder per ad:
`~/Library/Mobile Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/Mahj Meta Ads/`, plus
`contact-sheet.png` of every final. Each folder has a `<id>-copy.txt` with the
same copy as below, for pasting. Rebuild everything with
`python3 build_pack.py` (about a minute); `--all --out <dir>` also renders
the three cut candidates (C5, C6, C8); `--check <dir>` re-runs the pixel,
length, em dash, price and card-hand checks. Copy lives in `ads.py`; edit it
there and re-render, never in the PNGs.

Every image is built from real free-user captures of the app (iPhone 17 Pro
simulator, 2026-10-15) and the app's own tile art, fonts and colours. Every
rack shown is an app-generated practice deal or one of the app's original
teaching examples, never a hand from the NMJL card.

## Ads Manager upload checklist

Prerequisites (see `../meta-ads.md`, "Turning it on"): the Meta app for
`com.jackwallner.mahj` exists, the build with `META_APP_ID` is live on the App
Store, the App Store privacy labels are updated, and Jack has approved a
budget. None of that is true on 2026-10-15, so this pack waits.

**Campaign**
- [ ] Objective: **App promotion**. Campaign name: `Mahj US W40+ installs`.
- [ ] App: Mahj Trainer (iOS, App Store ID `6790052126`), store Apple App Store.
- [ ] iOS 14+ campaign / SKAdNetwork on (the app already ships Meta's SKAN ids).
- [ ] Advantage+ campaign budget off; budget set on the ad set.

**Ad set (one)**
- [ ] Performance goal: **Maximize number of app installs**. Switch to the
  StartTrial app event only once it has about 50 events a week (meta-ads.md
  step 8).
- [ ] Budget: about $20/day, daily. Schedule: no end date; review at day 7.
- [ ] Location: United States.
- [ ] Audience: **women, 40+, no interests, no lookalikes.** Under Advantage+
  audience only location and minimum age are hard limits, so open "Further
  limit the reach of your ads" (or switch to original audience options) and
  set age 40 to 65+ and gender Women there. Otherwise Meta treats them as
  suggestions.
- [ ] Placements: **Advantage+ placements** (Facebook and Instagram Feed,
  Stories, Reels and the rest). Do not hand-pick.

**Ads (one per concept, five in the ad set)**
- [ ] One ad per folder: C1, C3, C2, C7, C4. Never put two concepts in one ad
  and never duplicate one concept with a new colour: Andromeda would merge them.
- [ ] Identity: the Mahj Trainer Facebook Page and Instagram account.
- [ ] Single image or video ads: upload both ratios in **one** ad and use
  "Customize placements" so 4:5 serves Feed and 9:16 serves Stories and Reels.
  For C3 set each video's thumbnail to its matching `cover` PNG.
- [ ] Carousel (C2): upload cards 1 to 6 in order, 4:5 set; turn off
  "Show the best-performing cards first" and the end card; set each card's
  headline from the card list above.
- [ ] Paste primary text, headline, description and CTA from this file (or the
  folder's `copy.txt`). Add the two alternates under "Add text options".
- [ ] **Advantage+ creative enhancements: turn off** text improvements, visual
  touch-ups, image expansion, add overlays, music, 3D animation and image
  brightness/contrast. Every word and crop is sized for 50+ eyes and the 9:16
  safe zone; Meta's rewrites and crops undo that. Leave "relevant comments" on.
- [ ] Deep link: none (the store page is the destination).
- [ ] Preview every placement in Ads Manager before publishing: no caption,
  button or profile overlay on any text. All 9:16 text sits in x 90 to 990,
  y 290 to 1240; only the app screen runs below.

**After launch**
- [ ] Read at day 7 per ad: cost per install, thumb-stop (3-second views for
  C3), CTR. Pause an ad only on its own numbers, not on a two-day dip.
- [ ] If Ads Manager flags two ads as similar, pause C2 first (`scoring.md`).
- [ ] Refresh by adding a new concept from the cut list or a new capture,
  not by recolouring a live one.
