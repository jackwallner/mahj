Mahj TikTok: how to post

42 ready slideshows in Day 01 to Day 14, 3 a day. Each folder has 5 slides and caption.txt with the title, caption, pinned comment and reply. Day 01 is your first day after the warmup. Log results in Tracker.csv.

Set up once (7-day warmup, no app posts)

  1. Make 2 accounts on your own US iPhone. A personal name plus "mahj" works; keep "Mahj Trainer" out of the name. Real profile photo.
  2. Switch both to a Business account (Settings > Account), category Education. You get a bio link without 1,000 followers, plus analytics. The catch is the Commercial Music Library only, which disclosed posts need anyway.
  3. Bio: Mahj tips for new players. I made Mahj Trainer. Bio link, account A:
    https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6790052126?pt=128748779&ct=tiktok_a&mt=8
    Account B: the same with ct=tiktok_b. These show up as campaigns in App Store Connect.
  4. Twice a day, scroll For You for 10 minutes. Search "mahjong", "american mahjong", "mahj night", watch to the end, like, follow, comment now and then.
  5. Days 5 to 7: one non-promo post per account (your tiles, a snack spread). Ready when For You is mostly mahjong.

Post from a US phone, natively

  • Your iPhone, US SIM, home Wi-Fi or cellular. No VPN, scheduler or desktop upload. Posting from abroad sends the post to that country (one founder got 86% Romania and 0 trials).

Each post, about 3 minutes

  1. Files > iCloud Drive > Mahj TikTok Kit > today's folder. Select 1.jpg to 5.jpg > Share > Save 5 Images.
  2. TikTok > + > Photo mode > the 5 in order. Paste TITLE into the title field and CAPTION into the description.
  3. Sound: Commercial Music Library, the vibe in caption.txt, one with a trending arrow.
  4. More options > Disclose post content > Your brand. You made the app, so this is required (FTC). TikTok says the label doesn't limit reach.
  5. Post, then comment the PINNED COMMENT yourself and pin it.
  6. For the first hour, reply to every comment. Paste the REPLY on every "app?" or "what's it called?". Never post a URL in comments.
  7. Always write the exact name Mahj Trainer. Searching "Mahjong Trainer" or "mahj app" shows competitors first.

Schedule

  • Morning and Evening on account A, Midday on B. About 8 am, 12 pm, 7 pm Eastern.
  • Formats rotate A to G, so each gets 2 tries in 5 days. The CTA alternates inside each format: "comment app" vs "name it in the caption". caption.txt says which.

Measure trials, not just views (fill Tracker.csv at 24 h and 72 h)

  • TikTok analytics: views, saves, "app" comments, profile views.
  • RevenueCat: trial starts per day against your average for the 2 weeks before posting. Most trials start on install day, so a post that works shows up within 24 to 72 hours. Write the extra trials in trials_next_72h.
  • App Store Connect > Analytics > Acquisition > Campaigns: tiktok_a and tiktok_b downloads. A campaign appears after 5 downloads, a day late.
