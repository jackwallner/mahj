Mahj TikTok: how to post

42 ready slideshows in Day 01 to Day 14, 3 a day. Each folder has 5 slides (1.jpg to 5.jpg) and caption.txt. Log results in Tracker.csv.

Days 1 to 7: warm up both accounts (no app posts)

  1. Make 2 accounts on your own US iPhone: personal-style names, no "Mahj Trainer" in the name or bio, a real profile photo.
  2. Twice a day, scroll the For You page for 10 minutes. Search "mahjong", "american mahjong", "mahj night" and watch to the end, like, follow a few, leave a real comment now and then.
  3. Days 5 to 7: post one non-promo slideshow or video per account (a game-night snack spread, your tiles). Nothing about the app.
  4. If the For You page is mostly mahjong and game-night content by day 7, the account is ready.

Post from a US phone, natively

  • Your iPhone, US SIM, home Wi-Fi or cellular. No VPN, no scheduler, no desktop uploads. One account per app session.
  • Posting from outside the US sends the post to that country's audience (one founder got 86% Romania and 0 trials).

Each post, about 2 minutes

  1. Files app > iCloud Drive > Mahj TikTok Kit > today's folder. Select 1.jpg to 5.jpg > Share > Save 5 Images.
  2. TikTok > + > Photo mode (not video) > pick the 5 in order.
  3. Add a sound in TikTok (see caption.txt for the vibe; pick one marked trending, under 30 days old).
  4. Paste the caption from caption.txt. No link, no "link in bio".
  5. When someone comments "app", reply with the name: Mahj Trainer on the App Store. Never post a URL.

Schedule

  • Morning and Evening posts go on account A, Midday on account B. Rough times: 8 am, 12 pm, 7 pm Eastern.
  • Formats rotate A to G so each one gets 2 tries in the first 5 days.

Judge formats with devmelv's rule (fill Tracker.csv at 72 hours)

  • Under 3k views after two tries: drop that format. Skip its remaining posts and post another format's instead.
  • Over 10k views: it's a winner. Ask Claude for 3 to 5 variations (same format, new hooks and tips) and post those next.
  • Judge by the typical post, not the best one. Saves and "app" comments matter more than likes.
  • Formats: A wish-I-knew list, B mistakes, C quiz with a real rack, D Charleston explained, E vocab, F etiquette, G getting better.

Images: free FLUX renders and public-domain (CC0) photos for scenes, real Mahj screenshots for every tile and screen. Rules on the slides are standard NMJL play; none quote the current card.
