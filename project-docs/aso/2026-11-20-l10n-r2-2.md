# Localized metadata, round 2 batch r2-2, 2026-11-20

Seven Nordic and Central European locales rewritten field by field: da, sv, no, fi, cs, sk, hu. Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and description changed in each (name kept in sv, cs and sk because it already fits). Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Written against round 1 and its "Independent review" list (calques, tu/vous mixing, regional words, keyword repeats) and the r2-1 note.

## Where these locales can earn anything

The iTunes lookup for app 6790052126 returns a result only in Norway (no). Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Czechia, Slovakia and Hungary return nothing: the EU trader status finding in the r2-1 note (`TRADER_STATUS_VERIFICATION_STATUS_MISSING` for DNK, SWE, FIN, CZE, SVK, HUN) applies. So `no` is the only live locale of the seven; the other six are dormant and are written to be correct the day the territories open. Their rank cannot be measured (the app is absent from every result list).

## How the storefronts index these locales

Apple's table (developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations, fetched 2026-11-20): each of the seven storefronts indexes English (U.K.) plus its own language, nothing else (DNK: English U.K. + Danish; SWE: Swedish + English U.K.; NOR: English U.K. + Norwegian; FIN: + Finnish; CZE: + Czech; SVK: + Slovak; HUN: + Hungarian).

Design rules that follow:
- None of these seven is indexed by the US, so there is no US repetition rule. The only second pool per storefront is en-GB.
- en-GB (r2-1) already carries `american mahjong`, `learn`, `rules`, `charleston`, `practise`, `practice`, `beginner`, `tutorial`, `lesson`, `coach`, `quiz`, `nmjl`, `mahjongg`, `majong`, `joker`, `rack`, `strategy`, `free`. No keyword here repeats any of them (checked by script against en-GB name, subtitle and keywords: no overlap in any of the seven). So the local fields add the local-language words and a few loanwords people type that en-GB lacks (`offline`).
- Because `charleston` and `american` are covered by en-GB visible text, they were not spent in local subtitles; local keywords carry the local word for American (`amerikansk`, `amerikkalainen`, `americký`, `amerikai`), which combines with `mahjong` from the name.

## Method and limits

- Autocomplete: `MZSearchHints` for DK 143458, SE 143456, NO 143457, FI 143447, CZ 143489, SK 143496, HU 143482; 17 to 21 seeds each (prefix seeds `mahjong a/b/g/l/n/o/p/r/s/t`, plus the core words for rules, learn, beginner, practice, tiles, game, free, offline, American in the local language).
- Competitors: iTunes Search API titles and ratings (200 results, our app 6790052126); subtitles read from the App Store product page.
- Astro was not available on this machine, so there are no popularity scores. Picks rest on autocomplete, result-set sizes and competitor gaps.

What the research showed, and it is the same in all seven storefronts:
- Local-language autocomplete is almost empty. The only local-language hints that appeared: `mahjong gratis` (DK, SE), `gratis mahjong spel` (SE), `mahjong zdarma` (CZ), `mahjong ingyenes` (HU), `mahjong offline` (HU), `mahjong free` (NO, HU). Everything else is solitaire titles (`mahjong blast`, `vita mahjong`, `mahjong solitaire`, `mahjong club`, `mahjong soul`) and a few American-mahjong titles on `american mahjong`. No hint for rules, learn, beginner, tiles, practice, American in any of the seven languages. An empty hint is not evidence of no demand.
- Learning result sets are tiny, so ranking is reachable: `mahjong regler` 3 results (DK, SE), 6 (NO); `mahjong säännöt` 3; `mahjong pravidla` 3; `mahjong pravidlá` 2; `mahjong szabályok` 3; `mahjong nybegynner` 5 (NO); `mahjong begynder` 1 (DK); `mahjong nybörjare` 3; `mahjong aloittelija` 3; `mahjong začátečník` 3; `mahjong začiatočník` 2; `mahjong kezdő` 3; `amerikansk mahjong` 1 (da, sv, no), `amerikkalainen mahjong` 1, `americký mahjong` 1 (cs), 0 (sk), `amerikai mahjong` 1; `mahjong øvelse` 1 to 3; `mahjong harjoitus` 0; `mahjong cvičení` 2; `mahjong gyakorlás` 3. The same two or three apps fill these lists (Riichi trainers and one Mahjong scoreboard), all with 0 ratings. No one targets American mahjong in these languages.
- Tile queries are solitaire territory (`mahjong brikker` 58 to 62 results, `brickor` 74, `laatat` 42, `kameny` 23, `kamene` 16, `kövek` 10) led by apps with hundreds to thousands of ratings. Tile words are in keywords only as cheap single tokens, not as the lead.
- Competitor subtitles (read from the product pages): Mahjong Mind "Rules, Scoring, Riichi Trainer" (English in every storefront except SE: "Regler, poäng, Riichi-träning"), PVMahjong "Play, Learn & Improve Mahjong" (SE: "Spela, lära och förbättra"), Mahjong & Mahjong "Learn rules, yaku & scoring" (SE: "Riichi, regler och poäng"). Outside Sweden they have English subtitles, so a native subtitle already has less competition. Their words: rules, learn, scoring, Riichi, tutor.

## Not resolved, for the reviewer

- Six of the seven locales are dormant until the EU trader status is fixed (see r2-1). Only no can be measured, and only in Norway.
- No popularity data. Local autocomplete is nearly empty, so the picks are weak evidence: small result sets and a clear gap (nobody targets American mahjong in these languages). fi, cs, sk and hu have no local hints at all and are thin evidence.
- Native review is needed for all seven descriptions. Specific points: da `udlagt joker` and `udlæg` for an exposed joker and exposure; sv `exponerad joker`; no `eksponert`; fi `esillä oleva joker` and `esillepano`; cs and sk `vyložený žolík` and `vyložená kombinace`; hu `kitett joker`, `kitett sorozat`, `megtartom vagy eldobom`; cs and sk `odhoz`/`odhod`; the Apple account wording (`Apple-id`, `Apple-ID`, `Apple ID`) which Apple has been renaming to "Apple-konto" and similar in newer iOS versions. I kept `Apple ID` forms because they are universally understood and consistent with earlier rounds.
- The room headings keep the English in-app name in brackets, as in it and nl-NL, because the app UI is English-only for these seven languages; the descriptions say so in the second paragraph. Remove the sentence if these languages ship.
- The descriptions name card sections already in the live en-US description (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons). No hand from the card is printed.
- Name check, `itunes.apple.com/search` with the exact name per country: no other app has the exact trackName in dk, se, no, fi, cz, sk or hu.

## Check

`scripts/check-aso-l10n.py da sv no fi cs sk hu` is clean: every field is inside its limit, keywords are 95 to 100 characters, no keyword repeats a name or subtitle word, no duplicate, no plural pair, no `app`, no em dash. A separate script found no keyword shared with the en-GB name, subtitle or keywords. `scripts/validate-aso-metadata.py da sv no fi cs sk hu` passes. `asc migrate validate --fastlane-dir fastlane` returned `valid: true`, `errorCount` 0, `warnCount` 0.
