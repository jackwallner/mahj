# App Store metadata pass, 2026-10-28

This pass covers en-US, en-CA, es-MX and English search terms in the US-indexed ar-SA, ru, zh-Hans, zh-Hant and ko keyword fields. No App Store Connect changes were made.

## Method

Ranks are a snapshot from Apple's iTunes Search API, checked against app ID 6790052126 with `entity=software&limit=200` on 2026-10-28 Pacific time. Autocomplete came from Apple's MZSearchHints endpoint. The rank is the app's position in that response, not a popularity estimate. The [App Store search page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) says search considers the app name, subtitle, keywords and category. Apple's [localization reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations/) lists the US-indexed additional locales, and [localized app information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/localize-app-information/) says localized keywords can be searched where that language is supported.

The US storefront indexes Spanish Mexico, Russian, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Arabic, French, Portuguese Brazil, Vietnamese and Korean alongside US English. en-CA is a separate target for Canada. The full US SERP check used the current English and American Mah Jongg word pool, followed by checks for autocomplete-backed education terms and the previous `education` recommendation. Canada and Mexico were checked in their own storefronts.

After: `majong,joker,mahjongg,nmjl,charleston,tutor,beginner,lesson,rule,hand,quiz,tutorial,coach,teacher`

Three weak terms, `tile`, `flashcard` and `drill`, plus US `strategy`, were traded for `quiz`, `tutorial`, `coach` and `teacher`. The four removals are 28.6% of the old 14-token field. No title or subtitle changed. Keyword rank movements remain possible; ranks above are a pre-release snapshot and metadata has not been uploaded.
