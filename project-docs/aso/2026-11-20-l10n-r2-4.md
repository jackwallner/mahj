# Localized metadata, round 2 batch r2-4, 2026-11-20

Six locales rewritten field by field: ja, th, id, ms, ar-SA, he. Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and description changed in each (name unchanged in ja, ar-SA, ms: it already fits and ranks). Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Written against round 1 (`2026-11-20-l10n.md`) and its "Independent review" list: one address form per locale, no calques of the English name, the in-app term for each feature, no keyword that repeats a name or subtitle word.

## How the storefronts index these locales

Apple's table (developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations, fetched 2026-11-20):

- Japan: Japanese plus English (US). So ja and en-US are one pool in Japan; ja keywords are Japanese and add nothing English that en-US already has.
- Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Israel, Saudi Arabia: the local language plus English (U.K.). en-GB (round 2 batch 1) already carries `practise,practice,beginner,tutorial,lesson,coach,quiz,nmjl,mahjongg,majong,joker,rack,strategy,free`, so none of those English words are repeated in th, id, ms, he or ar-SA. A token check of every locale against its pool found only the brand words `mahj`, `trainer` (and `mahjong` in the id and ms names).
- United States: also indexes ar-SA (not ja, th, id, ms or he). ar-SA's keyword field therefore also serves US search, as round 1 did for the other US-indexed locales. The US English tokens already spent in en-US and the nine US-indexed fields (coach, game, quiz, learning, teacher, school, academy, 2026, free, offline, rack, dot, bam, crak, tutorial) are not repeated; the two added are `101` and `online` (US autocomplete: `mahjong 101`, `nmjl online`, `mahjong online`).
- The app is live (iTunes lookup returns it) in jp, th, id, my, sa, il and us. US is 96% of proceeds; none of these six is a revenue storefront, so the aim is local rank where the result sets are small enough to win.

## Method and limits

- Autocomplete: `MZSearchHints` per storefront (JP 143462, TH 143475, ID 143476, MY 143473, SA 143479, IL 143491, US 143441), 15 to 40 seeds each in the local language, English and romanized forms.
- Ranks and result-set sizes: iTunes Search API, 200 results, our app 6790052126. `n=` is the size of the result set; `out` is absent from it. Snapshots of today's live metadata, not traffic.
- Competitor subtitles read from the product page for Japan. For the other five storefronts the learning apps are the same small group of Riichi trainers with 0 to 6 ratings, so only titles were noted.
- Astro was not available on this machine (no `~/ios/aso/tools/astro.py`, MCP not listening). No popularity scores: picks rest on autocomplete, result-set size and competitor gaps. None of this is proven to add installs.
- Competitor names appear only below as titles read from the store, never in a field.
- Name check: `itunes.apple.com/search` for each exact new name in its own storefront (limit 50). No other app has that exact trackName in jp, th, id, my, sa or il. (The id, th, ms, ar-SA and he queries return 0 to 1 results, our own.)

What the research showed across the batch: American mahjong is a niche in all six markets. In Japan the whole store is Riichi (autocomplete is `麻雀 点数計算`, `麻雀 初心者`, `麻雀 無料`, `麻雀 オフライン`, `牌効率`); `アメリカ式麻雀` returns one app (not us) and `アメリカ麻雀` 14. In Thai, Indonesian, Malay, Hebrew and Arabic the learning queries return 1 to 12 apps (see each locale), so a keyword that matches both words of a query ranks first or close to it.

## Not resolved, for the reviewer

- No popularity data; every pick is autocomplete, rank, result-set size and competitor gap. The strongest evidence is the near-empty learning queries in Thai, Indonesian, Malay, Hebrew and Arabic; the weakest are ms, ar-SA and he (no or almost no autocomplete).
- Native review is needed for all six descriptions. Points to look at: ja `ラック` and `チャールストン（最初に3枚ずつ牌を渡す手順）` (the gloss is mine); th `แร็ค` for rack and `ไพ่` for tiles; he `מעמד האריחים (rack)` (no in-app Hebrew to check against) and `קיר` for wall; ms `jubin` and `rak`; ar `الحامل` follows the in-app Arabic (`قراءة الحامل`).
- The Hebrew spelling change is the largest expected effect in the batch and the riskiest: if Israelis mostly type `מאג'ונג` with the apostrophe form we kept in the keywords, the old name was already right. The result-set sizes (121 against 10) say `מהג'ונג` is the common one.
- The Japanese subtitle uses 18 of 30 characters and ja keywords drop every Riichi-only scoring term on purpose; Jack may prefer to chase `麻雀 点数計算` volume, but the app cannot serve it.
- The English UI note is in ja, th, ms and he; id and ar-SA need none (app languages: English, ar, de, es, fr, id, vi). Remove it from a locale if its language ships.
- The descriptions name only the card sections already in the live en-US description (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons). No hand from the card is printed.
- `Mahj Trainer: ...` is kept in every name.
