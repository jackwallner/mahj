# Localized metadata, round 2 batch r2-5, 2026-11-20

Six Indian-language locales rewritten field by field: hi, bn-BD, gu-IN, kn-IN, ml-IN, mr-IN. Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and description changed in each. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Read round 1 (`2026-11-20-l10n.md`, "Independent review") first; the checks it lists (calques, mixed address, keyword repeats) were applied here. Other locale folders, shared files and the round 1 note are untouched.

## Which storefronts index these locales

Apple's table (developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations, fetched 2026-11-20):

- India (143467): English (U.K.), plus Bangla, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu. So all six locales here are secondary keyword fields of one storefront, together with en-GB and the other Indian locales (or-IN, pa-IN, ta-IN, te-IN, ur-PK are in another batch). One keyword pool: a token repeated across any of them adds nothing.
- Bangladesh: the table has no Bangladesh row, and the iTunes lookup for our app returns no result with `country=bd`. So bn-BD (a locale named for Bangladesh) is only reachable through Bangla speakers on the India storefront. Treat it like the others.
- The US storefront does not index any of these. The app is live in India (lookup returns it), Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Nepal.
- Mahj Trainer is 96% US proceeds. These six locales will not move revenue much; they exist so a Hindi or Bangla reader of the Indian storefront sees native copy. Keywords carry both native-script words and the few English tokens Indian searches actually use.

## Method and limits (all six are thin evidence)

- Autocomplete `MZSearchHints` on storefront 143467, about 90 seeds: English (mahjong plus learn, tutorial, rules, coach, trainer, quiz, practice, charleston, beginners, tiles, offline, free, game), and native-script seeds for mahjong, learn, game, rules, practice and tile in all six languages.
- Indic-language mahjong terms return nothing in autocomplete for bn, gu, kn, ml, mr. Hindi returned `महजोंग` only as one competitor title ("mahjora: महजोंग सॉलिटेयर"). Hindi general words returned `सीखें` (programas: कोडिंग सीखें, immerse: भाषा सीखें), `गेम`, `नियम`, `टाइल` (pearl mahjong: टाइल सॉलिटेयर). Bangla `গেম`, `গেমস` returned hints; Marathi `शिका` returned `मोरू - मराठी शिका marathi`. Everything else is empty, and an empty hint is not evidence of no demand.
- Search API (200 results, `country=in`): native-script mahjong queries (`महजोंग सीखें`, `মাহজং`, `માહજોંગ`, `ಮಹ್ಜಾಂಗ್`, `മഹ്ജോങ്`) return 0 apps. `महजोंग` returns 144 apps, all Western solitaire (Mahjong Blast 11,395 ratings, Triple Tile 5,339) with English listings. `मह जोंग` (with a space) returns 143, same. So no app is competing in any Indian language for American mahjong, and the native fields are an open lane with little search volume behind it.
- English ranks in India now (our en-US listing shows there): `mahjong tutorial` 4, `mahjong lessons` 3, `mahjong trainer` 5, `mahjong tutor` 5, `mahjong practice` 33, `mahjong for beginners` 48, `mahjong rules` 57, `mahjong charleston` 38. Out of 198: `learn mahjong`, `american mahjong`, `mahjong coach`, `mahjong quiz`, `mahjong tiles`. English India autocomplete: `learn mahjong`, `mahjong coach: learn & play`, `mahjong trainer: learn & win`, `mahjong tutor` (PVMahjong, MCR), `mahjong quiz` (riichi, flush), `mahjong offline`, `mahjong 13 tiles`, `mahjong tiles`, `american mahjong academy`, `american mahjong: mahj lessons`.
- Competitors (India, `learn mahjong`): MAHJ ("Learn American Mah Jongg Cards", 2 ratings), PVMahjong ("Play, Learn & Improve Chess", 0), The Mahj App ("Classic NMJL Multiplayer Game", 0), Mahjong Mind ("Blind Timer & Home Game Ledger", 0). All English, all single-digit ratings. No competitor name is used in any field.
- Astro was not available on this machine; no popularity scores.

How that shaped the writing:

- The English words are covered by en-GB (r2-1: `practise, practice, beginner, tutorial, lesson, coach, quiz, nmjl, mahjongg, majong, joker, rack, strategy, free`, name `American Mahjong`, subtitle `Learn the Rules and Charleston`). None of those is repeated here. The two English extras that India autocomplete supports and en-GB lacks, `game` and `tiles`, are in the hi keywords.
- The six languages are one pool. hi and mr share Devanagari, so shared loanwords were given to one field only (hi: `जोकर`, `टाइल`, `कोच`, `कार्ड`, `गेम`; mr: its own spellings `रॅक`, `क्विझ`, `ट्युटोरियल`, `हँड`, plus Marathi words `सराव`, `खेळ`, `डावपेच`, `मोफत`, `शिकणे`, `धडा`, `पाठ`). `scripts/check-aso-l10n.py` was not used for the verdict: its word splitter cuts Indic words at every vowel sign and gives false "repeats a name word" hits. A whole-token check (NFC, split on spaces and punctuation, across all six plus en-GB) was run instead and is clean.
- The Mahj+ heading and in-app feature names stay in English in the descriptions with a native heading and the English name in brackets for rooms, because the app UI is English-only (app languages: English, ar, de, es, fr, id, vi). Each description says so in one plain sentence.
- Rack, tile, joker, dragon, wind, flower, crak, bam, dot are written as loanwords in the local script, the way Indian players and the earlier copy wrote them. Room, hand, quiz and account likewise.
- Numbers are ASCII digits throughout so card names (2468, 13579) match the card.

## Per locale

### hi (Hindi, India)
- Name `Mahj Trainer: महजोंग अभ्यास` to `Mahj Trainer: महजोंग सीखें` (`सीखें` is the evidenced learn word). Subtitle `टाइल, चार्ल्सटन रणनीति गाइड` to `अमेरिकन नियम और चार्ल्सटन पास` (`अमेरिकन` makes `अमेरिकन महजोंग` with the name; `नियम` has a hint).
- Spelling: `महजोंग` is the one spelling seen in a store title. Hindi `माहजोंग` and `मह जोंग` returned no hints; `माहजोंग` is used in the mr name.
- Keywords (99): `अभ्यास,शुरुआती,गेम,टाइल,जोकर,रैक,रणनीति,क्विज़,ट्यूटोरियल,गाइड,कोच,खेल,मुफ़्त,कार्ड,हैंड,game,tiles`. `गेम`, `टाइल`, `मुफ़्त` have hints in Hindi apps. `game` and `tiles` are the English India hints. Dropped the old `डॉट,बैम,क्रैक,ड्रैगन,विंड,फ्लावर`: no demand evidence, and `bam/dot/crak` are US-only terms. Dropped `पंग` (pung): no evidence.
- Evidence strength: thin. Copy uses `आप`, `ऐप`, `अकाउंट`, matching how Indian apps write it.

- Evidence strength: thin. Do not expect search volume.

## Not resolved, for the reviewer

- No Indic language has autocomplete evidence for mahjong. Keyword choices are the language's own words for the learning intent plus common loanwords, not proven demand. Expect near zero installs from these six fields; they are low risk.
- Native review wanted for Kannada and Malayalam (I am least sure of idiom there: `ಇಡಬೇಕೋ ಎಸೆಯಬೇಕೋ`, `ತೆರೆದಿಟ್ಟ ಜೋಕರ್ ಜೊತೆ ಬದಲಾಯಿಸಬೇಕು`, `സൂക്ഷിക്കണോ കളയണോ`, `തുറന്നു വെച്ച ജോക്കർ`). Hindi, Bangla, Gujarati and Marathi read as ordinary app copy but also benefit from a native pass.
- `Mahj+`, `Play a Hand`, `Mahj Minute`, `Fix My Mistakes` and the other feature names are kept in English because the app UI is English-only. Update the descriptions if these languages ship.
- Spelling variants (`मह जोंग`, `মাহজোং`, `મહજોંગ`, `ಮಹಜಾಂಗ್`, `മഹ്ജോംഗ്`) are guesses at how people type the word; one per language, unevidenced.
- English tokens `game` and `tiles` in hi may also be added by the other Indian batch (ta, te, pa, or, ur-PK share the pool); a repeat there only wastes bytes.
- r2-1 found the EU not on sale; India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal are on sale, so these six are live-reachable.
