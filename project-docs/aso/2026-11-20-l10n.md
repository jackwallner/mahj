# Localized metadata from keyword research, 2026-11-20

Ten locales rewritten: es-MX, en-CA, fr-CA, and the US-indexed secondary locales zh-Hans, zh-Hant, fr-FR, pt-BR, ko, vi, ru. Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and description changed in each. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Screenshots, release notes, URLs, en-US and every other locale are untouched.

Where the value is (last 90 days to Nov 19): US $2,906 of proceeds and 1,438 downloads, then Canada, Mexico. Search is about 81% of installs.

## How the storefronts index these locales

Apple's table (developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations, fetched 2026-11-20):

- United States: English (U.S.) plus Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), French, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico), Vietnamese. So zh-Hans, zh-Hant, fr-FR, pt-BR, ko, vi, ru, es-MX and ar-SA are extra US keyword fields; fr-CA is not one of them.
- Canada: English (Canada) and French (Canada) only. en-US is not indexed there. So en-CA and fr-CA are one keyword pool for Canada, and the two fields must not repeat each other.
- Mexico: Spanish (Mexico) plus English (U.K.). en-GB carries the same English keywords as en-US, so English terms are already covered in Mexico and es-MX should be Spanish.

Design rules that follow: English tokens are used once across the nine US fields (a repeat adds no coverage); Canada's two fields are split by language with English extras in fr-CA; the US-indexed locales keep native name and subtitle and spend keyword budget on distinct US terms plus their own language.

## Method and limits

- Autocomplete: `MZSearchHints` per storefront (US, CA, MX, FR, BR, KR, VN, RU, CN, TW, HK), 20 to 60 seeds each. Autocomplete outside the US and in non-English languages is thin; most local seeds returned nothing, and an empty hint is not evidence of no demand.
- Ranks: iTunes Search API, 200 results, our app 6790052126. `out/N` means absent from N results. Snapshots, not traffic.
- Competitor subtitles read from the App Store product page.
- Astro was not used: the MCP endpoint (127.0.0.1:18089) was not listening on jmbp2 and `~/ios/aso/tools/astro.py` does not exist there. No popularity scores, so choices rest on autocomplete, ranks and competitor gaps.
- Cross-locale combining (name from one locale, keyword from another) is assumed from the 10-28 note that `mahjong tutorial` ranks through the pt-BR and fr-FR fields. Not re-proved here.
