# Localized metadata, round 2 batch r2-3, 2026-11-20

Seven locales rewritten field by field: pl, ro, hr, sl-SI, el, uk, tr. Name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and description changed in each (ro keeps its name `Mahj Trainer: Învață Mahjong`; it already fits). Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Written against round 1 (`2026-11-20-l10n.md`, including its "Independent review" list) and the r2-1 note.

## Where these locales can earn anything today

The r2-1 finding applies here too: the app is not on sale in the EU (missing trader status). iTunes lookup of app 6790052126 returns a result in ua and tr, and nothing in pl, ro, hr, si, gr. So:

- tr and uk are the only live storefronts in this batch. Both have 0 to 3 ratings for every competitor that matters and tiny result sets, so rank is within reach.
- pl, ro, hr, sl-SI and el are dormant until the trader declaration is fixed in App Store Connect (Jack's decision; not touched). Their ranks cannot be measured. They are written to be right the day the territories open.
- hr also feeds Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and Serbia, and el also feeds Cyprus, per Apple's table.

## How the storefronts index these locales

Apple's table (developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations, fetched 2026-11-20): Poland, Romania, Croatia, Slovenia, Greece and Turkey each index their own language plus English (U.K.). Ukraine indexes English (U.K.), Russian and Ukrainian. The US does not index any of pl, ro, hr, sl-SI, el, uk or tr, so none of these is a US field and the US-only English repeat rule does not apply.

Design rules that follow:

- Every keyword token is checked against the other fields of its pool: en-GB (name, subtitle, keywords) in all seven, plus ru for uk. A script (in `/tmp`, not committed) found one repeat, `american` in ro (en-GB name has it), and it was dropped. Clean now.
- English search terms (tutorial, lesson, beginner, coach, quiz, joker, rack, free, practise) live once, in en-GB, and are not repeated here. `mahjong` plus `american` already come from the en-GB name, so local fields spend budget on the local words that combine with them.
- uk avoids Russian tokens already in the ru field: `офлайн`, `бесплатно`, `джокер`, `тренажер`. Words that differ in spelling (`безкоштовно`, `стійка`, `кістки`, `стратегія`) are kept because they are different tokens.
- English words are added to a local field only where en-GB lacks them and a hint or rank exists: `offline` (PL hint `mahjong offline`; US and AU hints too; ru has it for UA).

## Method and limits

- Autocomplete: `MZSearchHints` for PL 143478, RO 143487, HR 143494, SI 143499, GR 143448, UA 143492, TR 143480. Between 14 and 30 seeds each, in the local language plus English.
- Ranks and result-set sizes: iTunes Search API, 200 results, our app 6790052126.
- Competitor subtitles read from the App Store product page.
- Astro was not available, so no popularity scores. Picks rest on ranks, result-set size and competitor gaps.

What the research showed, per language: autocomplete for learning terms is empty almost everywhere. Every local-language seed (rules, beginners, learning, lessons, American, how to play, free) returned no hint in pl, ro, hr, sl-SI, el and tr. The hints that did come back are solitaire titles (`mahjong blast`, `mahjong classic`, `mahjong solitaire`, `vita mahjong`) and English fragments. All seven are therefore marked thin evidence. The one local-language hint set is Ukraine, and it is Russian (`маджонг без интернета`, `маджонг без рекламы`, `маджонг онлайн`, `віта маджонг`).

## Description decisions (all seven)

- The app UI is English, so each description says so in the second paragraph, as round 1 and r2-1 did, and keeps the English in-app names (Tile Room, Card Room, Charleston Room, Table Room, Play a Hand, Mahj Minute, How to Play, Endless Practice, Fix My Mistakes, Game Night Prep, Timed Challenge, Master Tables, Mahj+). Room headings show the English name in brackets so a reader finds it in the app.
- Built from the current en-US description. Card sections named are 2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons, which are already in the live en-US text. No hand from the card is printed.
- Informal second person throughout (`ty`, `tu`, `ti`, `εσύ`, `ти`, `sen`), no mixed address. Terms, privacy and the Settings path use each language's iOS wording (for example `Ustawienia → Apple ID → Subskrypcje`, `Ayarlar → Apple Kimliği → Abonelikler`).
- Mahj+ block states the 7-day trial and the Lifetime option as in en-US.
- No em dash, emoji, competitor name or "not X but Y" construction in any field (checked by `grep` and by reading).
