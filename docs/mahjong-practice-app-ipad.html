Is there a mahjong practice app for iPad?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

Yes. Mahj Trainer is a free American mahjong practice app that runs on iPad as well as iPhone. It drills the tiles, the card's sections, the Charleston and keep-or-throw calls in five-minute sessions, with the reason shown after every answer.

It needs no opponents, no account and no internet connection, and four practice rooms are free.

How does Mahj Trainer work on iPad?

Mahj Trainer is one app for iPhone and iPad. On iPad the layout was built for the larger screen:

  • Short questions and racks sit in the middle of the screen instead of being pinned to the top, and the flashcard deck keeps a readable width.
  • The app runs in portrait and landscape.
  • At large accessibility text sizes the home screen and the room grids fall back to a single column.

The layout was checked on iPad mini, iPad Air 11-inch and iPad Pro 13-inch, in portrait and landscape and at several text sizes. The app needs iPadOS 17 or later.

What can you practice on iPad?

Four free rooms, each a short drill on one skill:

  • The Tile Room: flashcards on every tile family, then a quiz on the rules new players mix up.
  • The Card Room: flashcards on the card's sections, then racks of 13 tiles where you name the section they are chasing.
  • The Charleston Room: practice deals where you pick three tiles to pass and compare with the coach. More on the Charleston practice page.
  • The Table Room: keep-or-throw calls and table rules.

Also free: one Play a Hand a day, where you draw and discard from a real wall while the coach grades every throw; the daily Mahj Minute, five questions a day that add up to a monthly challenge score; How to Play; and a searchable reference and glossary.

Where does it run?

Mahj Trainer also runs on iPhone and on Android. The Android page lists what differs there. Progress stays on the device you practice on.

Mahj Trainer at a glance

Mahj Trainer is a free app for learning American Mah Jongg with the yearly NMJL card, and it needs no account. Its quizzes explain the reasoning behind every answer.

Free from the first launch: the four rooms (Tile, Card, Charleston, Table), one Play a Hand a day, How to Play, and the glossary, plus the Mahj Minute on iPhone and iPad. Mahj+ adds unlimited Play a Hand, extra practice sets in all four rooms, Endless Practice with a new rack every time, Fix My Mistakes (your misses come back on a spaced schedule), Game Night Prep (a five-minute session built from your misses), a 90-second Timed Challenge, and the Master Tables for advanced Charleston, defense, expert rack reading, and joker rules. Mahj+ is a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Questions

Does Mahj Trainer work on iPad?

Yes. It is one app for iPhone and iPad, and on iPad it runs in portrait and landscape.

Is Mahj Trainer free on iPad?

Yes. It is free to download, and the four practice rooms, the daily Mahj Minute, How to Play, the glossary and one Play a Hand a day are free. Mahj+ is an optional upgrade.

Does Mahj Trainer work offline on iPad?

Yes. Every drill works with no network connection, and your progress stays on your device.

Does my progress sync between my iPad and iPhone?

No. Progress is stored on each device and does not sync between them.

Does Mahj Trainer play full games on iPad?

No. It is a practice app with no opponents. Play a Hand deals you thirteen tiles and grades every discard, one free hand a day.
