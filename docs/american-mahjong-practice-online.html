    Questions

    Can I practice American mahjong online for free?

    Yes. This page deals 13 tiles from a full 152-tile set, takes you through the three passes of the first Charleston and checks each pick against a coach. It is free, with no account and nothing to download.

    What should you pass in the Charleston?

    Pass the tiles that fit none of the sections your rack leans toward: lone winds, lone dragons and numbers far from the rest of your tiles. Keep pairs, and remember that jokers can never be passed. The Charleston guide covers every pass.

    Does it use this year's card?

    No. The card is copyrighted and changes every April, so nothing here quotes its hands. The coach reads the sections every card is built from (2468, 13579, 369, Consecutive Run, Like Numbers, Winds & Dragons, Singles & Pairs, Quints and the year hands). Keep your own card next to you for the exact lines.

    How do I type my rack into the reader?

    Any way that names each tile. Codes work (5D is 5 Dot, 2B 2 Bam, 7C 7 Crak, N E W S for the winds, RD GD SO for the dragons, F for a flower, J for a joker), and so do words like "five dots", "north wind", "soap" or "3 jokers". Share all 13 tiles for the best read.

    Is this a full game against the computer?

    No. It drills two decisions, what to pass and which section a rack leans toward. For whole games against bots or other people, see the comparison of American mahjong apps.

    Does the page keep my tiles?

    No. The drill and the reader run in your browser, and the tiles you deal or type are never sent anywhere.
