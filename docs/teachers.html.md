## What can you use in class today, with no code?

Three things are free for everyone and need no account:

- [Charleston drill and rack reader](https://jackwallner.com/ios/mahj/american-mahjong-practice-online.html): deal a rack on any screen, pass three tiles and compare with a coach.
- [Rules guides](https://jackwallner.com/ios/mahj/learn/): how to play, the Charleston, reading the card's sections, jokers, calling and scoring, plus a glossary.
- [The app's four beginner rooms](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahj-trainer-mahjong-practice/id6790052126?pt=128748779&ct=jw-mahj-teachers&mt=8): tiles, card sections, the Charleston and table rules, with the reason shown after every answer.

## Does it replace the card or a class?

No. Students need their own current card and a teacher. Mahj Trainer never shows hands from the card. It drills the skills that fade between lessons: recognizing the sections, choosing a pass, and deciding what to keep.

## Questions

### Is Mahj Trainer free for mahjong teachers?

Yes. Teachers of American mah jongg get Mahj+ free for life, and their students get their first month of Mahj+ free. There is nothing to pay and nothing owed in return.

### Does Mahj Trainer pay teachers or run an affiliate program?

No. There is no commission, referral fee or affiliate program. The offer is the free membership, the student codes and the printable cards.

### Does the app reprint the National Mah Jongg League card?

No. Mahj Trainer never shows hands from the card. Its drills teach the sections every card is built from, the Charleston and which tiles to keep, so students still need their own current card.

### Do students need an iPhone?

No. Mahj Trainer runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, and the student codes work on both.

### What can students use without a code?

The four beginner rooms (Tile, Card, Charleston and Table), one Play a Hand a day, the How to Play primer and the glossary are free for everyone, with no account.

### How is a code redeemed?

On iPhone or iPad, open the App Store, tap your photo, then Redeem Gift Card or Code. On Android, open Google Play, tap your photo, then Payments and subscriptions, then Redeem code.
