How it's built and why it matters.

The Pitch

Most people learn bridge at a weekly game or a beginner class, then have no way to practice until the next one. The lesson fades, the auction gets scary, and the same mistakes come back.

Bridge Trainer takes the parts beginners need most and turns them into short drills you can run standing in line: count the points, choose a bid, plan the play, pick a lead. No opponents, no clock, and an explanation on every answer.

Bridge+

The four beginner rooms stay free. Bridge+ is for players who want more reps and more depth: $1.99/month or $9.99/year (both with a 1-week free trial), or $29.99 once for lifetime access.

Extra practice sets. A second set of hands and questions in every beginner room, using the same drills you already know.

The Master Tables. Conventions, advanced declarer play and defense, and duplicate strategy, added to through the year.

What Mattered

Five minutes, honestly. A session is short enough to finish while the kettle boils. Nothing in the app asks you to sit down for an hour.

Reasoning over scoring. Every answer explains the bridge logic, because a beginner who knows why they were wrong improves faster than one who just sees a score.

Real 13-card hands. Drills use full deals rather than abstract puzzles, so the judgment transfers to the table.

Free means free. All four beginner rooms are complete without paying. Bridge+ adds volume and advanced material rather than gating the basics.

Bridge Trainer teaches a beginner Standard American framework. Partnership agreements and conventions vary, so confirm what you play with your partner.
