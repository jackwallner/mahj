Mahj Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Mahj Trainer works without an account. Practice progress, streaks, review history, onboarding choices, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Apple (on iPhone and iPad) and Google Play (on Android) process Mahj+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies entitlement status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device context. To learn which screens lead to a membership, the app also sends RevenueCat counts of where and how often the Mahj+ offer was shown, the dates of the first offer and of a purchase, how many times the app was opened before the first offer, and which screen a purchase started from. It does not receive your practice content, name, or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Feedback and question reports

On iPhone and iPad, Send Feedback, the "Something wrong with this question?" link and the one question asked when a membership is ending send what you write straight to the developer. Supabase stores it. Each message also carries the app version, iOS version, device model, language and region setting, and whether you are a Mahj+ member. A question report adds the question as you saw it: its prompt, tiles, choices, the answer and your pick. No name, email or account is attached. Messages are read to fix problems and improve the app, and are kept only as long as that takes. Because nothing ties a message to you, email us what you sent and roughly when if you want it removed.

Ad attribution

If you install Mahj Trainer from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for themselves.

What we do not collect

No account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, tracking pixel, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local practice data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat retain transaction records as needed to process and restore purchases. Support emails and in-app feedback contain whatever you choose to send and are used to answer or act on it.

Children and changes

Mahj Trainer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
