## Is there a free American mahjong learning app with no subscription?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

**Yes. [Mahj Trainer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mahj-trainer-mahjong-practice/id6790052126?pt=128748779&ct=jw-mahj-free-american-mahjong-app&mt=8) is free to download, and its four beginner practice rooms stay free with no subscription, no account and no ads.** It covers the tiles, the card's sections, the Charleston and keep-or-throw calls, on iPhone, iPad and Android.

An optional upgrade, Mahj+, adds extra practice sets and advanced drills. It is sold monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase, and nothing in the four beginner rooms needs it.

## What is free in Mahj Trainer?

- **The Tile Room:** 12 tile flashcards and a 16-question tile rules quiz.
- **The Card Room:** 16 flashcards on the card's sections and 9 racks to read.
- **The Charleston Room:** 10 rules and strategy flashcards and 9 practice deals.
- **The Table Room:** 14 keep-or-throw calls and a 16-question table rules quiz.
- **How to Play:** a six-page beginner primer, available any time from Settings.
- **Play a Hand:** one coached hand a day, with every discard graded.
- **The daily Mahj Minute (iPhone and iPad):** five questions a day toward a monthly challenge.
- **The reference:** a searchable glossary and a page for every card section.

Every answer shows its reason, and progress, streaks and stats stay on your device.

## What does Mahj+ add?

- An extra practice set in each of the four rooms, using the drills you already know.
- The Master Tables: advanced Charleston, a defense school, expert rack reading and joker school.
- Endless Practice, which generates new racks so reading practice never runs out.
- Fix My Mistakes, which brings back what you missed on a spaced schedule, and a ninety-second Timed Challenge.
- Game Night Prep, a five-minute session built from your misses.
- Unlimited Play a Hand.

Monthly and yearly plans start with a one-week free trial, and the lifetime purchase is paid once. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

## Will the app ask me to subscribe?

It offers a free week of Mahj+ during setup, and a Get Started button skips straight to the free rooms. On iPhone and iPad, a trial offer sometimes slides up after a good result: a few times in your first two days, then every few days, then weekly, and every two weeks after that. Close it and keep practicing. A small Mahj+ card also sits under each result. The free rooms never lock and never expire.
