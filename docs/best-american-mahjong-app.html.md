## What each app is for

### Mahjong 4 Friends

The most-rated American mahjong app here. It plays the NMJL 2026 and Marvelous cards, plus Chinese and other 13-tile formats, online with friends or offline against AI players, with tips for new players during the game. Its purchase list includes Premium, NMJL Pro and ad removal, and the listing does not say what each adds.

### American Mahjong Practice

A practice table for the card. In my test its Suggested Hands list ranked every card line by how many of your tiles already match, re-sorted after each pass or draw, but nothing explained why a pass or discard was good. Bots play valid patterns and only declare Mahjong if you turn that on. The 2018 hands are free; the 2026 hands are a one-time purchase or come with the monthly plan.

### The Mahjong App

Full American mahjong with the current card, a tutorial, AI opponents at several levels and games with friends. Premium adds themes, the American Mahjong Society card and hosting multiplayer games.

### Real Mah Jongg

Plays the NMJL card plus The Big Card and Mah Jongg Press against live players, bots or both, with Hand Suggestions and a scorecard of hands you have completed. It starts with a two-week free trial and then needs a monthly subscription.

### Mahj Parlour

Combines teaching and play. Its interactive lessons run from the tiles through the Charleston, calling, jokers, reading the card and defense, and every lesson is free. You can set up a private table with friends, be matched with other players or play computer opponents, with no ads.

### MAHJ: Learn American Mahjong

A course: 18 step-by-step lessons with tile drills, a printable cheat sheet, a glossary, bot games and an AI tutor that needs internet. Lessons 1 to 3 and several tile drills were free in my test, and one purchase opens the rest with no subscription. It has the highest average rating here.

### Maylee

Hand-illustrated tiles, a tutorial, the official 2026 cards plus simpler Maylee cards for new players, and daily challenge hands, with no Wi-Fi needed. In my test its "% to Mahj" button laid your tiles over each candidate card line, the clearest hand-choice screen I tried. Two games a day are free.

### MahJongg4Fun

An online game with the NMJL 2026, Big Card and Mahjong Press cards, ranked and friendly matches, voice chat, card-completion tracking and a Beginner Hub with rule drills and a Suggest Hand helper. Play past the free tier is a membership.

### Mahjong Trainer: Learn & Win

A guided plan: 17 lesson topics, a lesson of the day, bots that explain each move, and the only Apple Watch app here. It teaches the families a card is built from and does not reproduce an official card. In my test onboarding ran past 25 screens before the free-trial offer.

### Bam Dot Crack

Built for a group that lives apart: private Friends' Tables, AI bots for practice, leaderboards and tournament-style rankings. The listing shows no in-app purchases.

### NMJL Online

The National Mah Jongg League's own online game, with seven AI players, a lobby for meeting players and AI players that take over if someone drops. Playing needs the physical card and an online game membership; the tutorial is open to everyone.

### Mahj Trainer (my app)

Drills, not games: four free rooms for the tiles, the card's sections, Charleston passes and keep-or-throw calls, with the reason on every answer, plus one graded practice hand a day. There are no bots and no online play, and the practice hands are original, so you still need the current card at the table. [What is free and what Mahj+ adds](https://jackwallner.com/ios/mahj/free-american-mahjong-app.html) is on its own page.

## Which should you pick?

- **Your group wants to play online together:** Mahj Parlour, Mahjong 4 Friends, MahJongg4Fun, Real Mah Jongg or Bam Dot Crack. NMJL Online if everyone has the card and a membership.
- **You want full games with the 2026 card and no subscription:** Mahjong 4 Friends, or American Mahjong Practice with the one-time 2026 hands.
- **You have never played:** MAHJ for a course, or Mahj Parlour for free lessons with games built in.
- **You know the rules and want to learn which hand to chase:** American Mahjong Practice, Maylee or Real Mah Jongg.
- **You had a class and want quick reps between games:** Mahj Trainer.

## Questions

### Which American mahjong apps have the 2026 card?

Mahjong 4 Friends, Mahj Parlour, Maylee and MahJongg4Fun name the 2026 NMJL card in their listings, The Mahjong App, Real Mah Jongg and NMJL Online list the current official card, and American Mahjong Practice sells the 2026 hands as a purchase. Bam Dot Crack's listing says your NMJL card is in the app. MAHJ, Mahjong Trainer: Learn & Win and Mahj Trainer teach the game without reproducing the official card.

### Can I play American mahjong online with my friends?

Yes. Mahjong 4 Friends, The Mahjong App, Real Mah Jongg, Mahj Parlour, MahJongg4Fun, Bam Dot Crack and NMJL Online all list private or invite-only games with people you know, and most fill an empty seat with a bot. The Mahjong App's listing puts hosting a game in Premium.

### Is there an official NMJL app?

Yes. NMJL Online is the National Mah Jongg League's own game. Its listing says you need the physical card plus an online game membership to play, and anyone can try the tutorial without one.

### Do I still need the paper NMJL card?

At a real table, yes: every winning hand must be on the current year's card, which you buy from the National Mah Jongg League. An app that shows the card does not replace it at an in-person game, and NMJL Online requires the physical card to play online.

### Are mahjong solitaire apps the same as American Mah Jongg apps?

No. Most apps that appear for "mahjong" are solitaire games where you match pairs of tiles. American Mah Jongg is a four-player game played against a yearly NMJL card, with the Charleston and jokers. Only apps built for that game are compared here.

### Can I play American mahjong offline?

Some apps, yes. The American Mahjong Practice and Maylee listings say no internet is needed, Mahjong 4 Friends plays offline against AI players, MAHJ's lessons and drills work offline while its AI coach needs internet, and Mahj Trainer runs offline with no account. Games against other people always need a connection.
