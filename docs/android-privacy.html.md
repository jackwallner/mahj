## Mahj Trainer Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Mahj Trainer works without an account. Practice progress, streaks, review
history, onboarding choices, and preferences are stored locally on your
device.

## Purchases

Google Play processes Mahj+ purchases on Android. RevenueCat verifies
entitlement status and processes an anonymous app user ID, transaction
and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device context.
RevenueCat also receives limited app-open and purchase-screen counts,
timestamps, and paywall impressions associated with that anonymous ID.
We use this information to understand purchase and restore flows, not to
track you across other apps. It does not receive your practice answers,
practice history, name, or email. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## What we do not collect

No account, advertising identifier, advertising SDK, tracking pixel,
location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. We do not sell
personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local practice data remains until you delete it or remove the app.
Google Play and RevenueCat retain transaction records as needed to process and restore
purchases. Clearing practice progress in the app deletes only local
practice data, not server-side purchase or event records. You can request
deletion of data held for us by emailing the contact below. Transaction
records may need to be retained to meet legal requirements. Support
messages contain whatever you choose to send and are used to answer your
request.

## Children and changes

Mahj Trainer on Android is intended for adults and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when
the app or its services change.
