## Is there an American mahjong practice app for Android?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

**Yes. [Mahj Trainer: Mahjong Practice](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jackwallner.mahj) is an American mahjong practice app for Android, available on Google Play.** It teaches the tiles, the card's sections, the Charleston and keep-or-throw calls in five-minute drills, with the reason shown after every answer.

It needs no opponents and no account, and four practice rooms are free.

## What can you practice on Android?

Four free rooms, each a short drill on one skill:

- **The Tile Room:** flashcards on every tile family, then a quiz on the rules new players mix up, such as which dragon goes with which suit and what jokers can do.
- **The Card Room:** flashcards on the card's sections, then racks of 13 tiles where you name the section they are chasing.
- **The Charleston Room:** practice deals where you pick three tiles to pass and compare with the coach. More on the [Charleston practice page](https://jackwallner.com/ios/mahj/mahjong-charleston-practice-app.html).
- **The Table Room:** keep-or-throw calls and table rules: when to hold a flower, when to swap for an exposed joker, which discards are safe.

Also free on Android:

- **Play a Hand, once a day:** thirteen tiles off a real wall. You commit to a section, then draw and discard while the coach grades every throw and shows the arithmetic.
- **How to Play:** a short primer for new players, one tap from the home screen.
- **A searchable reference and glossary.**

Streaks and per-room progress show which skill needs work, and a quick mixed session brings back the questions you missed first.

## What about privacy?

There is no account. Practice progress, streaks, review history and preferences are stored on your device. The Android privacy policy says the app has no advertising SDK and does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data. Mahj+ purchases are processed by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat. Read the [Android privacy policy](https://jackwallner.github.io/mahj/android-privacy.html).

## Mahj Trainer at a glance

Mahj Trainer is a free app for learning American Mah Jongg with the yearly NMJL card, and it needs no account. Its quizzes explain the reasoning behind every answer.

Free from the first launch on Android: the four rooms (Tile, Card, Charleston, Table), one Play a Hand a day, How to Play, and the glossary. Mahj+ adds unlimited Play a Hand, extra practice sets in all four rooms, Endless Practice with a new rack every time, Fix My Mistakes (your misses come back on a spaced schedule), Game Night Prep (a five-minute session built from your misses), a 90-second Timed Challenge, the daily Mahj Minute, and the Master Tables for advanced Charleston, defense, expert rack reading, and joker rules. Mahj+ is a monthly or yearly subscription, each with a free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, paid through Google Play.

## Questions

### Is Mahj Trainer available on Android?

Yes. Mahj Trainer: Mahjong Practice is on Google Play, and the same app is on iPhone and iPad.

### Is Mahj Trainer free on Android?

Yes. It is free to download, and the four practice rooms, How to Play, the glossary and one Play a Hand a day are free. Mahj+ is an optional upgrade.

### Does Mahj Trainer work offline on Android?

Yes. The drills and their explanations are built into the app, so practice does not need a connection, and your progress is stored on your phone.

### Does the Android app play full games against the computer?

No. Mahj Trainer is a practice app. Play a Hand deals you thirteen tiles and grades each discard, and there are no bots or opponents.

### Is the Mahj Minute free on Android?

No. On Android the daily Mahj Minute is part of Mahj+. On iPhone and iPad it is free.
