## What does Mahj Trainer teach?

The tiles, the card, the Charleston and keep-or-throw calls, in five-minute drills across four rooms. Everything below is free. Mahj+ adds more of it.

### Every tile family

Craks, bams, dots, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers, then quizzes on the rules everyone gets wrong.

### Read the card

Learn the sections (2468, Consecutive Run, 13579, Winds and Dragons, 369, Singles and Pairs) and how to spot them fast.

### Name the section

See 13 tiles and call the section they're chasing, over and over, until the pattern jumps out.

### Pass without panic

Work real deals: choose three tiles to pass, then compare with the coach's pick and the reasoning behind it.

### Keep-or-throw calls

When to hold a flower, when to swap for an exposed joker, what an opponent's exposure tells you, which discards are safe.

### The why on the back

Swipeable cards that explain the rule instead of just testing it.

### A coach for every discard

Thirteen tiles off a real wall. Commit to a section, then draw and discard for twelve turns while the coach grades every throw and shows the arithmetic behind it. No opponents and no bots, so nothing can feel rigged. One free hand a day for everyone.

### Look it up mid-game

A searchable glossary and a page for every section of the card, one tap from the home screen. Free for everyone.

### More reps per room

An additional practice set in all four rooms, with fresh questions, racks, and deals added through the year.

Included with Mahj+

### Past the basics

Advanced Charleston strategy, a defense school, and expert rack-reading drills.

### Practice between games

Streaks and per-room progress keep skills warm between weekly games.

## More info

How it's built.

### The Pitch

Most new players learn mah jongg at a weekly game, then have no way to practice until the next one. Lessons fade, the Charleston gets scary, and the card still looks like a wall of numbers.

Mahj Trainer is practice without a table: quick, interactive reps you can run anywhere. Think of it as practicing your serve instead of playing a match. Learn the tiles, learn the card's sections, work through real Charleston deals, and make the keep-or-throw calls that decide hands.

### Mahj+

All four rooms are free. Mahj+ adds more: monthly or yearly (both with a 1-week free trial), or a one-time purchase for lifetime access. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

**Extra practice sets.** A second set of questions, racks, and deals in every room, using drills you already know.

**The Master Tables.** Advanced Charleston strategy, a defense school, and expert rack reading for players moving past the basics.

## Questions

## Is there an app to practice American Mah Jongg?

Yes. Mahj Trainer is a free iPhone and iPad app that drills the tiles, the card sections, the Charleston and keep-or-throw decisions in five-minute sessions. It also includes Play a Hand, where a coach grades each discard.

## Is Mahj Trainer free?

Yes, the download is free and the four practice rooms, flashcards, rack reading and the reference are included. An optional Mahj+ upgrade adds extra practice sets and the Master Tables, and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

## Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no sign-up, and every drill works offline. Your progress stays on your device.

## How do I learn American Mah Jongg as a beginner?

Start with the tiles, then learn the sections of the card, then practice the Charleston and the keep-or-throw calls. Mahj Trainer is built in that order, and the site has free beginner guides to the rules, the card, jokers and scoring.

## Can I practice the Charleston on my phone?

Yes. The Charleston Room gives you real deals, asks you to choose three tiles to pass, and then shows the coach's pick and the reasoning behind it.

## Does Mahj Trainer include the official NMJL card?

No. It is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League. It uses original practice hands and teaches the section structure that repeats every year, so you still use the current official card at the table.
